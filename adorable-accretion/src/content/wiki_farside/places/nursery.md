**Location:** Old Farside

Metal and crystal plants line the inside of a large garden house. The plants here, just like the **soulful** machines are only discernable from their biological counterparts by their metallic appearance, as their function is the same. 
Small brush exist next to enormous metallic plants that grow from the same core from the ceiling. 

Containers litter the area, filled with many of the mysterious substances used.
Druids experiment here and grow the plants. 

There are small full metal insectoids that help plant seeds and help out the druids.
