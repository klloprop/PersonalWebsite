The original center of the Farside druids grove. A giant valley with luscious fields and forests reside in the center of Farside. Multiple smaller forests, more remnants of Old Farside can be found throughout the city. It is surrounded on all side by New Farside, the borders marked by enourmous metal walls that keep the valley warm.
