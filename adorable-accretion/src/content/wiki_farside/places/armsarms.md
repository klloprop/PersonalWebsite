**Location:** Old Farside

A prosthesis workshop in old farside. One of the oldest and most renowned metalcraft workshops in Farside.
This place was once a weapon smith for the heroes, now knights, and still has a deep connection to the Emerald Knights of Farside. 
Today this place produces almost only prosthesis.
