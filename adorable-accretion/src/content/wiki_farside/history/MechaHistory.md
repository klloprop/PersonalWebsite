Farside has been home to many breakthroughs and innovations.
The first metal creations were simple. Prosthetics, machines and structures counted amongst the first inventions, starting the age of the industrial revolution. Those early inventions, where dirty in their production and in operation, choking the streets with thick black smoke. 

These **soulless** machines are often regarded as lesser, for their dirtiness and their reduced functionality. 
The druids quickly found better ways, more expensive ways to create even more advanced machines. 

The druidic magic replaced the dirty combustion engine with something akin to a soul, allowing the metallic **soulful** creations to eat just like their biological counterparts, and burn those resources cleanly. 

The exact procedures to create such machine cores that can sustain **soulful** machines is only known to experts. We know it requires some level of druidic magic and a deep understanding of both crystals and metals as well as their interactions. 

We do know that **soulful** machines react to different kinds of metals being used in their design, some combinations of materials even granting them abilities, allowing them to mimic claws, bioluminescence and more.

Language has developed to speak about the level of technology in the everyday life in Farside.
**Naturalists** refer to individuals who do not use prosthetics, for one reason or another.
(German: **Naturalisten**)
**Semis** use prosthetics either because they are disabled to regain functionality or to enhance or gain new functionality. 
(German: **Teil-Maschine**)
**Full Metal** refer to full machines, both **soulless** and **soulful**. Soulless and Soulful always refers to the respective Full Metal machines.
(German: **Vollmaschine**)

Prosthetics today are usually only made with soulful technology if any is required.
