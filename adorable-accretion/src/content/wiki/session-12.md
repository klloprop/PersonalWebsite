(Without Kenny and Mik)

The session starts with two appetizer introductions. In one we see Guldor track down the Ivory Might in an attempt to find answer, however the interaction turns hostile before Guldor learns anything new. In the other intro we see an older Human figure that is identified as an Archmagus. This Archmagus is discussion Reverie and Aaron with another unidentified figure, apparently these people have been warding Reverie from curses. 

The core session starts by the party introducing themselves to Arneal Gleamlight, the mysterious contact they had been looking for. Arneal demands forthcoming and absolute honesty from the party. 

After much discussion the party settles on a plan. They want to turn the dominions against each other, all the while spreading word of the upcoming conflict towards the civilian population, hopefully starting a revolution.

Nailo learns more about the war of Shadow and Light and that Deathwalkers have been spotted on the fields in front of Menzo.

Carmelio has a emotional but overall mixed interaction with Arneal in which it is revealed that Carmelio has destroyed a village and killed many in an unexplained situation.

Reverie has a short conversation about the study ground with Arneal, but only learns that it possibly is the best guarded secret in the Starwielder dominion and that promising young mages are recruited to work there. Knowing that Aaron and herself where the two best students in her time at the academy she utilizes the sea of knowledge to figure out where Aaron is and learns he is leaving the city. She runs down toward the edge of Raphia desperate to meet Aaron before he leaves. With a natural 20 on the check Reverie arrives just before Aaron at the gate, sweating and out of breath. The following conversation reveals that Aaron has been looking for the truth behind Reveries family tragedy for all this time and he has been invited to work at the study grounds, finally having the opportunity to learn what secrets that thing holds. As the weight of their responsibilities and plans weighs down on the pair, their emotions run wild and they share their first kiss.
