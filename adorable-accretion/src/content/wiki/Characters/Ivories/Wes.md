**Pronouns:** He/Him

Akira's husband and a sweetheart.

He is an artificer who got kidnapped by the Ivory Dominion. Has been found in Aneridge under the supervision of a Sirenhead automaton. He is currently specialising in advanced mechanical weaponry, hoping that his invention can turn the tide in the war. Is working with Percival Mourningbloom.
