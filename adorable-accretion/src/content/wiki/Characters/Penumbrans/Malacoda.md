**Pronouns:** He/Him

A pure blood demon and a generally unsettling fellow. Mala had a dark and mostly unknown upbringing. He was involved in the bloodwar that rages between the demons and the devils below Umbra, eventually leading to Urduk cursing Mala. 

The curse decreed that Malas firstborn child, despite not being a full demon, can never feel the warmth of the overworld, rendering all of Malas efforts to escape fruitless.
Mala sought ways to dispel the curse, but in the end could only make a deal with a witch, namely the witch Alecto, to split that curse so both Nailo and Mala would bare halve.
