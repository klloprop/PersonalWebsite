**Pronouns:** (He/Him)

A starwielder mage and partner of Reverie. He met and saved Party in the Dust Well, while studying strange automatons below Raphia.

Then he obtained a position at the Study Grounds and later disappeared under mysterious circumstances.
Now trapped in the feywild he met the Nameless King and revealed his name to the fey as a bargain to surive. Forced to work under the king, he acts as the court mage and is taught and supervised by Malachi. Over time their relationship grows closer and more complicated.
Having learned how to wield dream magic, Aaron regularly meets Reverie in her dreams, helping her to the best of his abilities despite being duty-bound to the king.
