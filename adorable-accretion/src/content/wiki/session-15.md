(Without Kenny)

This session we start with the party recuperating after the fight with Pascal-Felipe, as Pandora sneaks into the camp to sow some mischief, she notices the wounded and even recognizes some of the people present. Not much later Pandora joins the party as Arneal returns wounded, urging them to head to Maplehorn immediately.

Maplehorn lays in a swamp and most of the construction is built in the water using wooden supports. The village itself is nothing special, a handful houses scattered around the main road, with the one notable exception of a pristine hotel in the middle of Maplehorn. 

Our party was told to head to the same hotel and once inside they discover that for each and every party member there is a room with their name written on the door using a metal plaque. Upon further investigation they find that Pandoras room is the odd one out, some text was scribbled on her rooms wall in large letters. Soon after a door appears and opens leading them to a graveyard. 

In said graveyard they find two graves, the graves of Ekira Danareius and Pheya Danareius. Pandora is drawn to them but cannot figure out why. Finally at the end of the graveyard they find the witches hut and enter one at a time or in pairs. Everybody is proposed a deal from the witch and one by one they all eventually decline, leaving with nothing but curse wards. Pandora does learn that before she lost her memory she was apparently the witches apprentice and that she had a sister.
Oh and also Arkus acts up and gets turned into a wooden doll.
