We start with a cold open between Faena and Alecto, The Witch. Old bonds are mentioned and it is eluded to the fact that Alecto raised Faena in some capacity, although their relationship seems to be unstable and Faena seems to harbor bad feelings. In their conversation it is mentioned that "they" would return and soon. In response the witch will relocate her hut and hotel to someplace else, leaving Maplehorn, come dawn, defenseless.

And as the sun rose and illuminated the swamp the hotel uprooted and walked on chicken-like legs. Faena stormed into the Town hall, dead set on killing the party here and now, however, the villagers do not take kindly to someone threating their heroes and interpose themselves. It quickly becomes clear that Faena has no intention of hurting the villagers, who use this fact to corner and trap the fey. Using this opportunity the party escapes Maplehorn.

After a moderate track they arrive in Vrocmore, a dead husk of a former city. This is apparently the city that started a great undead plague called the Aneridge scourge, which claimed over 25 thousand lives in Vrocmore and the neighboring city, Aneridge.
Noon was involved in the happenings, but is not yet forthcoming with that information towards the entire party.

Having to rest to prepare the proper illusion spell, the party makes camp on one of the many fields surrounding Aneridge, providing party with a splendid view of the marvelous landscape and, come dusk, the artificial sun that provides light in the night.

In the next morning the group makes it to Aneridge, making their way into the inner ring. Thanks to Masks powerful illusion spell, they could pass by the gates without issue.

Something is brewing in the air. Aneridge has already suffered a horrible once-in-a-lifetime tragedy not two decades ago. Mortals should really get out of the habit of calling things once-in-a-lifetime.
