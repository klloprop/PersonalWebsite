(without Mik and Hime)

Starting with a tense conversation between Nailo and Vhaerun, which ended with Vhaerung leaving but promising to stay close. Before the group departs, after the resting period, Noon and Mask have a short conversation, as well as Reverie collection information about the Oakheart from a local druid. 

Afterwards the party take a carriage north towards Raphia which takes them most of the way. Before reaching the captial, the group stumbles upon Torrine a small village which due to robots taking over the manual labor tasks here has been mostly abandoned. Here the group finds first magical iron residue of Raphias Iron Storms and meet Agdan Bagdal the local engineer and sole resident of Torrine. 

Continuing their journey they reach Raphia as the sun falls beneath the horizon, coming upon a group of Brightstriders having a short but tense conversation with one of the Paladins there.

Now as the group has arrived in Raphia they need to find masks to shield themselves against the metal dust and decide on their next course of action.
