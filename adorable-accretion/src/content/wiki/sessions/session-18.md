(Without Kenny, Mik showed up later)

Akira is holding Noon ever since she dragged the very injured tabaxi back inside. Their wounds are partially mended. After the worst is over Mask joins them, carefully putting one hand on each of them, staying with them. Akira tells Mask about the spirits that she is seeing, as the spirit of the undead cerivtaur sits with her and communicates that he is sorry. Akira can ascertain that he is from her own tribe and likely a trans-man as her tribe consists only of women. However his story and how he ended up in this swamp remains a mystery. Mask makes note of that ability, already sensing it is something very special, but needing to research the details later. The spirits fade as the storm ends.

Nailo and Vhaerun talk about what just transpired and about their fears regarding the war in penumbra. In that conversation Vhaerun takes Nailos hand to comfort him, but keeps it there after the moment has passed. They address their handholding briefly, but both seem to be enjoying it. **Nailo makes the decision to go to Menzo next.**

Ivory observes the two drow for a moment, looking at the wooden doll in his hands. Envy and longing nesting in his heart, but he just currently cant fully trust Arkus intentions. He does not know what is going on with the golden tiefling.

Reverie has a brief conversation with Aaron via their telepathic earrings. It turns out the study grounds are protected by many powerful wards and Aaron has to step outside to talk to Reverie. He tells her about large spell circles, their function yet unknown. Large amounts of magic flow in this place and many hallways and places are off limits to the young mage. Still, Aaron is determined to find out more about this place and return to Reverie. 

Finally Reverie uses the Sea of knowledge after talking with carmelio about the voice he has heard, trying to find out what the relationship between Carmelio and that entity is. She sees Percival Mourningbloom and a team of wizards trying to install a strange runestone into carmelios still lifeless body, but that specific operation fails. Unbeknownst to anyone, the runestone imprinted on the inside of Carmelios primordial engine, forming some sort of bond with that entity. It turns out Carmelio contains part of that entity within himself, making him, in some sense, part of that entity.

As the session draws to a close, a group of Stormchasers, armed with various weapons and large backpacks, can be seen entering the village. In their group a young owlin scribe with many books seemingly documenting the storm and its effects. Additionally Faena has somehow joined the group and is trying to keep a low profile until she has identified her exact marks. 

After the necrotic storm has passed many small orbs of residual necrotic energy float around the swamp, which draw many fey here. As the party finally leaves the town hall they witness a very rare occasion, hundreds of fey feeding on magic, a phenomenon that had not occurred in the last 6 years.

Reverie wakes in Carmelios arms after exerting herself with a use of the sea of knowledge. They talk about being more careful with her powers and that Carmelio could potentially be an anchor for Reverie when she uses the Sea of knowledge.

In the new day Reverie uses the Sea of knowledge to inquire about her parents, finding out that the Study grounds is some weird multi-dimensional place and also a prison. Build like the legendary panopticon this prison is weird and alien, additionally much magic flows through this place. Reverie sees her parents navigate the prison and eventually free a person from it, afterwards the vision cuts out.

Akira makes contact with dead cervitaur and eventually buries his remains. Additionally Akira and Noon finally have a hard talk about Noons past.
Mask cures Noons curse.

Nailo has another conversation with Vhaerun, in which he realizes he will go back to Penumbra, he needs to be with his family and needs to help with the war efforts in any way he can.

Arkus starts growing, slowly regaining control over his body and has a lengthy conversation with Ivory about very important things.

Pandora shows up at the old Bakery inquiring about their previous life and about their sister, learning she was the witches apprentice and that the witch is responsible for her sisters death. She was believed dead as well.

Just before Reverie goes to sleep for the night Aaron calls her and the pair share a short and sweet conversation.

In the evening Mask leaves the group to research spells and to find more information about Akiras sudden abilities. She eventually learns that Akira is something called 'Spiritbound'. Before she can head back to the group to share her insights, she is cornered by a dark skinned fey called Faena. Faena quickly makes it clear in no uncertain terms that she is here to kill Mask and all of their friends. After a short conversation mask gets punched, but finds out that Faena is likely scared of the witches influence in Maplehorn, prompting Faena to try to drive the group out of the village.

After Mask shares what has happened with the group, they decide to stay the night and decide on further action in the morning.
