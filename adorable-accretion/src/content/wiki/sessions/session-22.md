(With Everyone)

Cold Open between Mavari and Ramira
We start the session with a cold open between the sisters Mavari and Ramira in their Quarters within the Citadel in Aneridge. It is revealed that Ramira shows a special aptitude for primordial magic and is the top student of the Technomancer program. The young tiefling however has reservations about their current training regiment, questioning why they want her to destroy automatons. 

Back in the safehouse Reverie awakens, trying again to discerne what is happening with Aaron, but as she enters the Sea of Knowledge she looses herself. In the end it is a bottomless sea, accepting of all that there is and all that can be. And so it took part of Reverie, showing her a deep-seated wish, her family, alive and united at a single dinner table. Only when Aaron joins them, covered in some deep black liquid, Reverie knows something is wrong. Eventually she manages to pry herself free from that space, to leave the Sea of Knowledge once again. But what was left behind?

Seeing as Reverie falls unconscious and does not awaken again, Noon and Carmelio freak out, trying in vain to help Reverie. Eventually it is concluded that she is safe, well, as safe as she can be and that she will awaken again. 

Sasha made his way to the library with Nailo, Vhaerun and Astraea, where Sasha went off on his own to research all the news from the past few weeks, learning that his absence has not gone unnoticed and that he has been deemed missing, with the Starwielder Dominion now looking for him. In an attempt to stay undiscovered he devises a new infused concoction, a sort of deodorant that does well at hiding your magical trace and your scent in addition to the curse-ward the party had procured from the witch. 

Back in the Library Nailo and Vhaerun talk about Vhaeruns magic. The young drow states that he does not want to lie anymore, but it is the only thing he has every been any good at. In the end the pair agrees that for the time being "good intent justify the means".

Akira, Pandora, Wes and Arneal get to reunite and talk for a short bit, before Faena interrupts them and prepares for an attack. However, before Faena can she the first blood the group is surrounded by cloaked figures that turn out to be advanced (second Generation) Automatons in addition to a human, General Paragon. General Paragon has followed Wes here and is looking to bring him back. 

A large fight ensues that quickly sees our heroes in great peril. Akira tries to flee with Wes on foot but is repeatedly stopped by automatons, Faena and Miria Paragon, brining Akira down to low hp. During this fight a unknown humanoid cloaked in many Brighstrider garbs joins the fight and helps out our heroes. Eventually Arneal manages to grab Akira and Wes, fleeing the fight. Pandora and Vesper, the Cleric that has joined the fight, manage to flee shortly after, leaving Faena and General Paragon to duel it out.

Arneal lands somewhere in the outer ring, transforms back into his 'normal' owl form and the group makes their way back into the city and towards the safehouse. 

Pandora and Vesper retreat to a tavern to plan their next steps. 

Everyone except for Pandora and Vesper eventually arrive back at the Safehouse.
