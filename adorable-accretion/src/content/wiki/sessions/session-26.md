(Without Mik)

We start with a cold open watching Faena speaking to her father for the last time, a giant bear called Father Hunt. The Nameless King is present as the First fey prepare to retaliate against humanity as the Emerald grove is still burning. It is eluded that both of Faena parents, Mother Nature and Father Hunt died during this conflict.

The Session starts with the party in the empty lower artifact chamber and Arkus and Dorchiot just a few floors below them on the Upper Ring. With Reveries magic the party learns that Arkus could escape.
As the alarm bells ring out the party splits up. Vhaerun, Akira, Noon and Nailo head up to the higher artifact chamber to search for the artifacts, while the rest of the party heads downwards to reconvene with Arkus and attempt to exit this place.

Making their way down the Citadel the group manages to pass as confused politicians and bankers until they run into Pascal felipe. A spicy lie from Arkus deters most of his men from pursuing the fleeing group and a few very cruel and effective spells incapacitate the recovering Golden knight. 

The smaller party makes their way through the prison, evading Hallorn the Faceless and finds and frees Faena who is being interrogated by Todoryk, a Sage of the Brighstrider Dominion. Eventually they make their way to the Upper artifact chamber which turns out to be the Dragon kings hoard. Volnescend, Grace of Kings, Last of the Dragonkind, greets them through gritted teeth, but the group manages to convince the Dragon to let them pass into the chamber beyond. 

Inside they find Merith disguised as Guldor staring at a intricate device surrounded by many artifacts of fable and legend. Faena and Merith share a hushed conversation about the significance of the 6 scaled eggs in the device. Merith seems shocked and scared at the scene before them while Faena seems excited, ready to head back to the feywild to inform the Nameless king of the human transgressions.

Merith asks the party for aid to try and stop the apocalypse should the Nameless king return to the mirror realm. Unsuccessful to retrieve an egg from the machine the party manages to flee from the Citadel as the session ends.
