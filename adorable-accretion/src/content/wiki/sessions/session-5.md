(without Kenny and Mik)

On the search for a lead to find the mysterious alchemist and artificer duo, the group braves a descend into the Dustwell. The way down proved to be more difficult than anticipated. The narrow path made mostly of scraps affixed to the wall of the Dustwell gave way, revealing an alternative path through buried and abandoned buildings. Following the alternative path, the group stumbled upon a the next of scrappers, intelligent birds that use the scrap in the Dustwell to create armor, decoration and their nests. The heroes soon learned that these birds are extremely territorial. A fight ensued resulting in one dead scrapper and the party fleeing from the nest. Eventually they all made it to the bottom of the Dustwell. Now they have to navigate the iron-dust filled ruins and debris.
