The session starts in the ritual room after the recombination ritual, fully recombining all of Alianas soul shards.
After the ritual a storm starts outside, characteristically for this region of Abaron, the storm featured illusory components, making it seems like the rain is falling upwards, despite the windows still being pelted by droplets of water. 

The party returns to their rooms, gathering in one of them to discuss further plans. Carmelio examines his curseward here and finds that it has been tampered with, likely by Maleth.

Aliana joins the party and offers a wish to everyone present. Reverie asks for the promised solution to the Sea of Knowledge. Carmelio asks for a new curse ward. Nailo asks about a solution to his personal curse, but Aliana would irreparably damage his soul if she were to remove the curse forcefully. Mala, later in private, asks the same questions, offering his own life and safety but Aliana has to dissapoint Mala once more. 
Arkus akss for a favour and for his brother to be cured of Taeyars madness later. 
Noon wants to talk to Vrij. Akira ask for a weapon, a dagger, to kill anything forever, destroying the soul. 

Mask waits for her wish, finally and secretly asking for that which she desires most. 

Aliana reveals how she travels the SOK freely, she used the madness to split her soul over and over again and protecting each piece in an additional protective layer made of other souls. This allowed it to get resistance against the tearing pull of the Sea of Knowledge. 
This Lichdom ritual is explained and Reverie eventually decides to perform it aswell. Her soul is split into 5 pieces as she is plunged into that wide sea over and over again. To stabilize the soul Aliana put 4 soulshards into special containers. For the time being Reveries memory will be fuzzy.   

Noon talks to Vrij, learns Guldor is alive, that her village was atttacked and that some kids survived and are hiding in caves nearby. 

In the later part of the session, during the night the group hears loud sounds originating from Alianas room. Once they enter the room Aliana is sitting in a corner, a window is open and aliana is unable to talk. The party tries to uncover what has happened here and what the unknown attacker tried to achieve.
