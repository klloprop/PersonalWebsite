The mystery continues. As the session starts the party finishes investigating Alianas room. 

Noon eventually removes the everfrost from Aliana allowing the party to ask further questions about the attack. Aliana shares everything openly, except the exact history or value of her amulet, only stating "it was a gift from my mom".

Carmelio and Reverie leave the others behind in search for someone that can tell them more about the piece of cloth that they found on the broken chair which likely belongs to the attacker. They quickly run into a tired pera in the hallways, who happens to be head of the student council and can pinpoint the piece of clothing to the first floor where the first year students reside. 
Since the attacker jumped out of the window to escape being caught, Reverie and Carmelio start searching the room positioned underneath that window on the first floor. 

Nailo is suspicious of the amulet and eventually convinces Aliana to surrender it just long enough that Mask can cast identify on it. Completing the ritual cast reveals shocking information, the amulet holds a very powerful curse of control, which would have allowed someone to control Alianas every move, had she put on the amulet. Further a powerful protection spell, a delayed blast fireball hides inside the amulet which is triggered upon tampering or destruction. 

Carmelio and Reverie find the attackers room. The room is lived in and has been used for a while. They find coded correspondences, armor, weapons and the original necklace taken from Aliana hidden in a wooden box. 

Carmelio takes potions from the wardrobe and Reverie grabs the letters from the desk before they meet up again with the rest of the group. 

Noon identifies the attacker to be a tabaxi from her place of birth, a village inhabited soley by tabaxi. 

A smart application of the scrying spell allows Reverie to track down the tabaxis location. Both Aliana and Noon, with a haste spell and the tabaxis unnatural speed, go after the tabaxi and eventually catch up.

While Aliana is walled of by everfrost, Noon can close the gap and tackle the other tabaxi to the ground. 

A fight ensues... next session.
