Having just escaped the automaton onslaught, they still needed to face the oncoming storm. A quick search later they find a cave, which turns out to be an old mine entrance. As they clear the entrance the heavy rain soaks our heroes. They just barely escape the static electricity and the following rain of thunder. Once inside the cave they rest and wait for the storm to pass, most of them in more revealing outfits. 
After the storm passed they made their way to the derrailed train carriages finding very little except a toolbox that seems to have some significance to Akira. 
Afterwards the party headed to Oakheart to rest as the sun was already rising again. Picking up a color-splattered Pandora on the way, they eventually find themselves in the Roots tavern, eating breakfast and soon after going to sleep.

Only Nailos rest period might be a little bit _more_ than he bargained for.
