*CLANG* 
Swords clash on the training grounds on the various red sand fields. Training dummies and stands holding several training weapons line the edges of the same fields.

Mask sits on a bench nearby, her long tail swishing through the red dirt, slowly staining her white fur brownish red. Her coat is still growing thicker and longer, a surefire sign that winter has fallen in the overworld. 

*CLANG*
Mask attention wanders back to the trio in front of her, Merith, Nazira and Noon. 
Merith favors light weapons, so she can utilize her gauntlet in the offhand, today she has chosen a short sword. Nazira, in classical Nazira-fashion, favors heavy weaponry and today she is wielding a comfortable greatsword. 

Noon is standing off to the side, her eyes examining their every movement. Mask really stands out in this little group, sure she could wield a rapier, but that looks more like childs-play next to these martial artists. 

*CLANG*
Merith encroaches on Nazira with a swift movement. Nazira tries to react, turning a dangerous situation into a opportunity, but as she steps back her wings try to open... but only one wing actually opens. Her balance is off and she stumbles backwards, falling on her ass.

"Enough" Noon exclaims, as Merith lowers her blade.
"I can do it" Nazira seethes, "AGAIN"
Noon steps closer, "Nazira." her tone is kind and sharp
"13 to zero" Merith adds with a mischevious grin, but she is quickly silenced by Noons glare.
"You need time to adjust to your new situation" Noon explains, "You need to walk, jump and run before you can fight... you lost a wing Nazira." she pleads with the devil.

Nazira slowly stands, kicking up a red dust cloud and growls, "Fine."
Nazira doesnt wait for the other two to make her way to Mask.

"Wipe that smirk of your face" Noon teases Merith.
"What? Can I not revel in being the undefeated champion?" her smile grows even wider. 
"Not undefeated" Noon mutters.
"That was a fluke! In fact we should clear this up once and for all. Right here. Right now."
"Mhhh" Noon steps closer, "You sure you can take me? You must be pretty exhausted" Noon purrs, her eyes scanning the blue scaled body. 
"I could take you in my sleep" Merith retorts, already taking a offensive pose.
"Fine then" Noon smiles,  grabbing a blade nearby. 

Their exchange is aggressive, at times reckless, as they try to land a hit on each other but always narrowly miss. 
As the swords clash, Nazira extends her wing, the large white feathers are covered in the red sand. 
"This wont come out for days..." Nazira sighs, "Im still not allowed to shower or take a bath, not until next week." she complains.
Mask observes her, "Does it hurt? Your other wing I mean"
"Not really, I more so forget its gone and THEN I hurt myself" 
"So it does hurt, just in a different way" Mask smirks.
Nazira snorts, "Sure does".

Naziras hands have started carefully combing through the feathers, trying to remove the red particles. 
"Mind if I help?" Mask asks, a idea already forming.
"Mhh? Eh, sure." Naziras own movements continue as her eyes examine Mask, who scoots closer and without warning takes of her shirt, revealing a utter mess of dense white spotted fur.
"Wha-?" Nazira mutters as Mask brushes herself against the wing, the fur doing a fine job of collecting all the red dust.
"But now you are dirty" Nazira cannot contain a chuckle, "Sorry this is just a delightful image"
"You are enjoying this?" Mask teases as she finishes the outside of the wing, "besides i don't mind as I can just take shower later. And there is not much to see here"
Then she gently lifts the large wings and scoots underneath it, her fur brushing against Naziras toned muscles. 
"MASK-" Nazira exclaims as a deep purple joins the colours in her face.
"What? I gotta clean both sides" still heat rises to her own cheeks as she brushes against the wing.
___ 
"I think the wing is clean, mask" Nazira says bashfully, her eyes locked onto the fighting pair.
"Yeah i think so too" Mask responds dryly, turning her head upwards to look at Nazira expecting more of a reaction.
"Heh, I see" Nazira chuckles shyly, wrapping both her wing and a arm around Mask, "How about this then?"
Before Mask can properly answer a low purr escapes her throat, as she buries her face into Naziras shoulder.
___ 
On the training field both the tabaxi and the blue dragonborn are panting heavily, sweat running down their bodies. Neither wants to admit defeat, but neither of them manages to land a clean hit either. Their extended exchange has, in fact, moved them off the actual training field and closer to the storage sheds nearby. 
Finally seeing a opportunity to pin down the smaller and faster tabaxi Merith attacks with rejuvenated ferocity, pushing Noon against a shed. 
In another swift move Merith strikes Noons blade, creating a opening which allows Merith to lunge closer, dropping her own sword and pressing Noons against the wall, her other hand landing on Noons shoulder. 

"Nowhere to run now" Merith manages to say, while heaving for air. 
"Oh nooooo, how tragic" Noon retorts between deep breaths.
"You little-" Merith cannot help but smile as she presses Noon harder into the wall.
Noons face immediately contorts at the stronger pressure.
Merith face drains of blood and she immediately eases off, "Are you alrigh-"
Using the opening Noons hands shoot forward, grabbing a dagger from Meriths waist and pressing it against her abdomen.
"You- You faked?" Merith stutters out, slowly stumbling back from Noon. 
"More importantly, I won" Noon smiles, "Two to Zero, and I believe that makes me the undefeated champion" Noon smiles widely and Merith cannot help but do the same
"Well played wildcat." 

"Wildcat?" Noon tilts her head.
"I- Forget I said that." Merith turns away from Noon, but she can still feel that soft inquisitive gaze on the back of her neck, "Urgh its just... you sometimes remind me of her. Faena." Merith suddenly blurts out.
"I'll take that as a compliment" Noons soothing voice breaks through the building panic in Merith.
"You better do." Merith chuckles, "I miss her. If only I could shown her..." she exhales for a long moment, "Now she's gone.".

"Now I am here" Noon puts a hand on Meriths lower back, "And Nazira and Mask"
"You know that that is different" Merith chuckles out, still avoiding Noons piercing gaze. Why do her eyes make her unravel so easily. 
"Is it?" Noon stands next to Merith now, "Look at them" she nods towards Nazira and Mask the latter almost entirely hidden from the world by the white feathers. "Look at us".

"Shouldn't you go over to fight over your girlfriend?" Merith exhales.
"I dont think so." Noon answers, "Mask and I talked about it. About Nazira and you"
"Am I- are we that obvious?" Merith, starts to relax a tiny bit.
"Is it bad to be obvious?"
"No- thats not-" Merith stammers out, as Noons hand travels up to Meriths face and gently turns her head so their eyes can finally meet.
"You are evil" Merith whispers against the tabaxis hand.
"I know, its one of my better qualities."
Meriths hand travels up to Noons ear, her hands reverently brushing against the fur, "Still 'wildcat' is for Faena, you are-"
"Noon is fine."
"Okay. Noon then."
"Want to join the others?"
"So *you are* getting jealous" Merith smirks as they walk off the field.
