**2190 - The Beginning**

I first met Nokomis when my father tended to business with the dryads. A tall and imposing woman. Her legs did not resemble those of a human, instead large cat-like paws and strong furred legs appeared underneath her dress. Her face was vaguely human, but her piercing eyes and the cat like ears marked her as clearly fey. 

First fey, my father told me. Not to be disrespected. 

Still when i stood there in that room i felt safe, her warm smile grazing me from time to time. Even when i grabbed for her tail she only smiled and let me play while they talked. My father apologized profusely which only amused her.

**4662 - Sister**

My parents visited Nokomis often and even let me stay over with her and the other dryads.
I remember the night when she first brought her own daughter. She must have been no older than a few weeks. 
Nokomis is holding the small child that much resembles herself. A tiny tail is wrapped around her hand as she holds the girl. The girls eyes are wide, taking in every little thing. 
"Mother Nature, what- whats her name" my tiny voice reverberates against the woven roots.
Nokomis leans down with a smile, "Her name is Faena and Aliana, please call me Nokomis"
"Nokom- Nokomth- Noko?" My hand reaches out, but Faena recoils deeper into her mothers embrace, which makes Noko laugh.
"If you want you two can play together from now on." Noko offers.

**8820 - Studies**

I stumble through the archway into Nokos den and plumb down on one of the soft root woven seats.
"Uuuuuuuurgh" I groan as I untangle my bag from my body. 
"AL!!!!" A small voice resonates from a nearby room and before I could react Faena had pounced on me, "Al! How are you, how are you? Why are you gone so horribly long every day."

"Fae-" I try to say but she jumps on my chest again, "Faena" I eventually chuckle out, a hand cupping her face and my finger rubbing her ear, "I study with the other dryads, they are teaching me about Arcana!!"
"Isn't that boring?"
"What? Its the greatest thing! I can already move rocks and-"

"Aliana" Nokos voice cuts through the the room, "I did not expect to see you here today"
I awkwardly turn my head to face Mother Nature, "Yeah, Mom said she would come by here later so I figured I would walk home with her."
She gracefully sits down next to me, her hand landing on my head, "I see, you are always welcome, Faena certainly has taken to liking you."

"I do not! hmpf" the little purring ball on my chest protests, which elicits laughter from both of us.
Suddenly the room feels very *very* small as a great figure steps into the room.
"Father Hunt!" I exclaim in surprise, trying to sit up.
"At ease" his voice bellows, as his large bear paws gently pick Faena from my lap. 
"It is good to see you Aliana" he adds before kissing Noko, "I will show Faena the outdoors" he adds, before leaving. Not exactly a man of many words.

"The druids tell my about your progress. They also told me about the fire."
"I- oh" I exhale, "They go so slow! And they dont teach me the things that actually matter I-" my eyes meet her keen kind ones, "I just want to learn at my own pace, I'm sorry about the fire"
"Its alright Aliana, I hear your complaints, but these things need time. Some things cannot be rushed."
"But I am ready!" 
"If you are so determined, then I will teach you from now on."
"Really?? Thank you!!" my singsong carries through the wooden halls.

**12002 - Graduation**

"Miss Rexum" I hear Nokos voice, but my eyes are stalwart faced towards the crowd in front of me

For your exceptional accomplishment to reliably draw arcana from the stars themselves and for broadening the arcane understanding of all of us-"
I feel Noko next to me now and I awkwardly turn to face her, "I am find myself with the greatest honor to bestow upon you a title worthy of your accomplishments."
She pauses, her eyes resting proudly upon mine for a moment before she addresses the congregation, "Please welcome the great mage Aliana Rexum, Wielder of the Stars"
Hesitant applause moves throughout the space. I see my parents and Faena in the front row beaming up at me, but I also see the other fey and their grim visages. 
"This isn't about them" Nokos whisper interrupts my thoughts, "This is about you, I'm proud of you kid" her hand lands on my shoulder as I face the crowd with renewed confidence.

**16972 - Loss**

It is a cold winter night when I stumble into the woven tree. My eyes are red, my vision blurry and my cheeks are stiff from frozen tears.
I am met by Faena, she must have heard me approaching. Before I reach her my knees buckle and I collapse on the ground. She is at my side immediately. In this moment I cannot help but ponder the strangeness of our situation. I am maybe a decade older than Faena and I am in my mid 40s while she looks only maybe 12 years old. Still in this moments she holds me together as I sob in her arms.

"What happened Al?" Faenas voice is shaking, I guess she has never seen me like this.
I open my mouth to answer but i can only choke out a cry as new tear start streaming down my face. 
"Aliana!" Noko appears in the room. 

...

It takes a lot of time before I am able to speak. Faena sits beside me holding my hand, and I crumble into her embrace more than once. Noko is moving around us, bringing tea and blankets. 
"They died last night." I eventually manage to say, "They are gone and I couldn't even say goodbye."
"Who died?" Faenas voice is small, she must be scared. I cant blame her. 
"My parents" my voice is hoarse as I finally form the words, "They died too soon, they were sick and I thought they were getting better... I was travelling..." tears stream freely once more.

Noko has settled opposite us, "Im sorry to hear that Aliana" her voice is weirdly calm.
"I could have done something, maybe I could have saved them." I cry out.
"Death is part of the rhythm of nature, Aliana" Noko says, "it is best to accept natures course."
"What?" my tone is exasperated "I could have saved them I-"
"They lived a long and full live. They accompanied you deep into adulthood. Savor the time you had with them."
"How can you be so cruel, so..." Im trying to contain myself, trying to not insult Noko.
"Mom." Faenas voice is uncharacteristically stern, "Not now, Mom"

"I see. I will give you some time, I will always be close" she stands, "Feel free to stay here for as long as you like. We have a room for you" she turns towards the door, but before she leaves she adds, "Aliana, I know with your arcane knowledge death might seem like a problem to be fixed. Nature should never be trifled with." 

"Im sorry, mom doesn't understand" Faena speaks softly after her mother had left the room, "She is speaking with little care but she does speak truth"
"Just- Stay with me?" I ask as I lay down on the couch.
"Of course."

**20000 - Observe**

Living with Noko, Faena and Venari (Father Hunt) has proven to be quiet pleasant. Faena is often out with her father, which leaves me and Noko to our own devices. We often spend time in the same room doing our own thing and occasionally discussing my latest discoveries.

I'm not sure Noko comprehends how old I am growing. At least she doesn't pester me about finding a partner. The feys speed of living life and their expectations do vary widely...

Despite my own studies, I find myself with a decent amount of free time, which I spend by following Noko to various meetings and galas. Meeting people is interesting enough, although it is weird that everyone has heard of me and yet I know nobody.

In these years I met a few interesting individuals, but I fail to make a meaningful connection with any fey. I get along splendidly with those they deem 'mortal', my own kind. Among these mortals, two in particular stand out, Turuk Brightstrider and Joras Ivory. 
I introduce myself as Aliana the Starwielder to all of them, now that my title has become more recognizable than my actual name. 

Of course there is one more notable individual. Noko calls them the Observer, a first fey, just like herself. They seldom appear in the same form twice, one day its a stately fey lady, another time a human duke. I quickly pick up on this individuals behaviors, eventually managing to spot them at almost every event. They rarely engage in conversation and true to their title they observe.

Feeling quiet out of place at many of these events, especially those dominated by the fey, I find myself gravitating towards this calmer familiar anchor. 
The first time I openly approached him, he had chosen the image of a younger fey man. Our conversation was short, but full. I did not expect anybody here to be able to match my intellect, but Servas (Observer) proved me wrong. With him seemingly attending every event in existence, we kept running into each other. After a while I noticed that he was changing his forms in a particular way.  Grant changes were replaced by smaller alterations of familiar forms. I eventually made him confess that he had taken an interest in me and was trying to find the form that most appeals to me. 

A sweet gesture. Little did he know that I had already decided to keep him around.

Our lives continued on, and I started making more connections with mortals. Eventually I stopped following Noko around and instead started planning my own galas and dances. I guess we started growing apart.

**23192 - Unnatural**

"Aliana! You must respect natures course!" Noko reasoned, her calmness bordering on something sharper.
"Why should I accept suffering and death when I can prevent it?" exasperation coats my voice, "I have accomplished more in my short life than most fey ever will. Why should I bow to natures whim?"
"Check your tone miss." there it is, the thorns, "remember who you are speaking to. Nature is not to be trifled with, not a problem that requires a solution"
"For fucks sake Noko, humans do not share your lifespan or your resilience or even power. I mean look at her" I point at Faena, she has been sitting quietly in the corner of the room, her ears are flat against her skull. She doesnt want to be here either, "She is still a teenager, im at the end of my life! We mortals simply do not have the luxury of time."

"Aliana" Nokomis voice calms once more, "There won't be a place for you here if you go down this path."
"I will not stand idly by and let my people suffer." I hiss.
Nokomis does not respond as she gets up and leaves the room.
"Im sorry Faena, I should not have dragged you into this" 
"Its okay. I understand" she stands and turns to follow her mother, "Just, don't be evil okay?"

"I won't- " I respond automatically, "but why do you side with her if you do understand?"
"Because I do have the gift of time, resilience and power. Because we are not the same and can never be." her voice is on the edge of breaking. 
I do not push her. 
I leave her be.
And turn towards my future.

**30102 - The Problem**

"Aliana?" Nokomis is the first to raise her voice. 
I wondered if she would recognize me. My gray hairs have vanished and my youth is once more on full display. I have defied nature, looking as old as Faena.
"What did you do?-" Her voice shakes, im sure she is disappointed.

"Mother Nature, I bring forth a issue from the Ai'ruma settlement." My tone is official, I am not looking to argue about my personal life, "the winter is harsh and our population is growing. We are in dire need of shelter, food and medicine."

It pains me to look at Faena, as she sits next to Nokomis. Maybe I feel guilt. 
"Aliana, I can redirect the druids attention, but we are already doing all we can. Nature will provide-"
"It is not providing, otherwise my people would not be starving. You forbid us from cutting woods for shelter and heat and you forbid us from hunting-" my tone is too harsh, filled with rage. I cut myself off.

"Nature is sacred and you will do no such thing." Nokomis states, "I know that this is a difficult winter and if we work together hopefully we will be prepared for future winters. Nature will provide for us all."

"We won't be happy with that answer forever." my mouth feels dry as I form the words. I do not mean it as a threat, but as a warning.

...

The winters only became harsher.

**36743 - The Fire**

The last time I step into that living tree, my old home, I am wearing my full uniform. That of a general, of THE Starwielder. It is late at night. My feet are heavy. I know what is to come. I cannot stop it.

"Mother Nature" I address her, she is seated in the living room.
"Aliana Starwielder" her voice lacks life, she too knows.
"The mortals are fed up with death and suffering. We will take land for agriculture, to grow enough food to sustain our population, we will harvest stone and wood to build shelter and we will grow gardens for herbs to make medicine for our sick."

"You know I cannot allow that" she sounds weak, worn out. 

"I am not asking. I am informing you."

"The dryads will-"
"I know." I interrupt her, "Take Faena and go. Leave this place to us. I will deal with whoever comes next."
"Aliana, I cannot do that."
"Why?" my composure fates and tear threaten at the corners of my eyes.
"I cannot abandon the Emerald Grove the same way you cannot abandon your people."
"So you would rather die?"
"Yes, it is in my nature."
"Is it also in your nature to watch your daughter die?-" 
"WATCH YOUR TONGUE !" her voice bellowsm, as her form changes. I guess I have never seen her angry.
"I will not let my sister die." I whisper.

Her arcane prowess rivals my own, but I was always faster.

The first rune-circle is formed before Noko can even react.
The dimension door leaves me standing in Faenas room, her sleeping form before me.
As I grab her the tree is already forming thorns. 
With minor injuries I complete the second rune circle. 
I suddenly stand in a glade, Faena in my arms as I cradle her. 
I slam my hand down creating a barrier, just in time as the ground rises up to consume us.
I have just enough time for the third teleportation circle. 

A faraway forest, out of her reach. I know Venari is close by, I have a way of knowing the unknowable. They shouldnt be able to make it back to the grove until morning, it should be too late. 

"Whats happening Al?" Faenas sleepy voice sounds out.
"Your father is close by, find him." I say, as tears roll down my face.
"What? Where is mom? What is happening?" her eyes are panicked now. I cannot help her.
"You will hate me-" Its hard to keep my voice from breaking, "I would hate myself too."
"WHY? WHAT ARE YOU DOING AL?" her scream rip through the forest.
"I- I love you Faena."
"Al, *please* I'm scared."
I do not have a response, so I just form the last teleport circle and vanish before her. 

*I underestimated the Hunts speed. Venari would make it to the grove before sunrise. He too would burn trying to save his love. If I had known I would have lured him even farther.*

As I teleport back into our camp the men stand ready. Turuk and Joras at their very front. They await my command and yet I could not stop them if I tried. 

I guess it is in our nature after all.

**40223 - The End**

It has been almost ten years since the Emerald grove burned. I haven't seen Faena since. 
I get out of bed, leaving Servas (The Observers) embrace and walk to the window. Golden rooftops shine in the early morning sun. The Star Disk floats gently nearby. A golden age, they call it, Ai'ruma at the center of it. 

But my eyes do not come to rest on the early traffic in the streets or the golden roofs, but instead on the small splotch of darkness visible on the horizon.
I know what it means.
I know of their king.

I inform Joras with a spell, evacuations are first priority.

"SERVAS" I scream as I throw a cardigan over my form, "He is here. Get out."
I don't wait for a reply as I storm out of our room.

It occurs to me while running down the hallways that teleporting would be faster... I guess it feels only right to let nature prevail one last time in these moments before calamity.
I reach the gardens, I always kept a piece of pure nature around, a place where magic wouldn't touch. I hastily cut off four roses and continue on my way down the ever cascading stairs of the academy. 
I rush past students whos head turn in confusion and fear. 
"TO THE TELEPORTATION CIRCLE ALL OF YOU" I scream, but I do not stop.

At the bottom of the stairs, I find the old tree. Still standing between two old abodes, remnants of the dryads. At the foot of this tree sit four clumsy carved headstones. Not my finest work, I must admit, but I made them by hand.

Two mothers and two fathers lay here. 
*A conjured rose would not have sufficed*, I think as I place one flower on each grave, *I think I did good.*

I don't have anything to say to them so I stand there for a moment, sweaty and still in my night gown as the bells start tolling. The evacuation is underway. Soon night will fall here and slaughter will follow.

I smile to myself, knowing I will not see the morning should I stay... still I cannot abandon my people, my mages and my city. 
"I guess its just in my nature-" I whisper.


Little did I know, 
that this very tree,
would burn so soon.
*What a cursed tree.*
