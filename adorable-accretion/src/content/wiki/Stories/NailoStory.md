**Written by: Kyrodot**

The details of the event were lost to Nailo as soon as he had been informed of them. Perhaps it was meant as an appeasement to the incessant crowd of nobles and their demands of revelry in midst of a war, perhaps it was a ploy set by his father to trick them into spilling their pockets into Menzos artillery. It mattered not, because Nailo would rather be anywhere but here, making small talk with nobles that all but sneered at his mere presence before them.

He supposed it was not all bad, however, as he watched the ragtag gang of misfits he'd found himself entangled with try some of the finer dishes their cooks had come up with, reactions varying across each face. They deserved a break from all that had been plaguing them, he mused as a rare genuine smile crossed his face.

Naturally, it would not last. The rhythmic click of shoes only became apparent when it was too late, seconds away before they came to a halt and mismatched eyes met a pair of greens. Darker, older, certainly not the pair that haunted him in his dreams for 15 years, but a near thing. Lady Vierna Vandree wore her grace as she always did, unashamed, relentless, as though there was little that could pull her down from the heights she strives for. Much like the emblem of her house, she is and always has been a snake. Nailo noted an unusual glee in her smile, never a good sign.

"Your highness, you look splendid, as usual." A pause, and an appraising glance. As though he was cattle. A pillars throne may have burnt, but their habits thrive as they always do, the prince mused to himself.

"Lady Vandree, it is an honor."
The dark haired lady's smile twitched, though her face did not move an inch otherwise.
"Oh please, the honor is all mine. It is not often the Royal house holds events these days, what with those pesky dominions knocking on our walls. I suppose it cannot be helped however" she feigns a long suffering sigh, the kind only an unblemished generational noble might make. Nailo's smile grew tight, going seemingly unnoticed by the ex matriarch.

"Peskiness is certainly one of their many traits. Nothing we cannot handle, however."
"Yes, I suppose you are correct in that statement. I have come to congratulate you on your victory many victories, especially against that... brute and his flying vermin. The gentle folk of Menzoberranzan sing tales of your skill and stretch them far and wide, my prince."
The previous sick glee reared it's ugly head yet again as she leaned in cospiratorily, as if Nailo was one of her many friends.

" Rumor is you even managed to bring my unruly son to heel."
The words were brief, spoken almost in jest. Yet the impact they left was an earthquake whose force shook the prince out of his polite restraint, turning his blood to ice and his tone to ash.
"Pardon me, my lady, I don't believe I heard you correctly." He managed to rasp out at least half confidently, he hoped.

The elder drow took a slow sip of her wine, a casual action that might've made anyone with a not so keen eye believe they were simply discussing the weather. The pause stretched as she swallowed the liquid, speaking in a tone that signalled she knew her victory neared.
"The people speak of my disowned bloods devotion to being your loyal hound. They say he sticks to your side as though he'd been welded to it, and earned a pardon for his efforts. It makes one wonder why the crown has had a change of heart. A sweetened deal, perhaps?"
Her smile did not reach her eyes, though it often did not, and she inclined her head, aiming for good natured yet landing on sickening as a pit formed in Nailo's stomach.

The shame came first, a searing pain that threatened to engulf your insides until they were palpable for the masses. Shame was his most loyal companion, his first lover, the devil on his shoulder. The weight sunk low and deep, and he wished the ground would swallow him whole so he did not have to be here, speaking to his evil wench that sought nothing but destruction for those around her if it meant she got to stand above them in victory.

He busied himself with his drink, delaying his confrontation with the fork tongued viper at his side as his eyes swiped across the crowd in a way he hoped looked leisurely. They found Vhaeraun and Kraz, because they always did. The two were chatting up a storm, the familiar leer in Vhaerauns eyes making it's appearance in response to something Kraz had blurted out, yet a tinge of affection graced his features despite the obvious frustration at his companions words. The succubus was entirely unaware, of course, rambling on about something or the other as he tended to. His friends were scattered across the crowd too, all in their own little bubbles of socialization, all with little weight to hold down their actions, to their words. They did not fear some measly noble wishing to restore her former glory so badly she would spout such vile accusations about her own flesh and blood. The realization hit him like a gladiator before he even had the time to catch up.

Years of training finally rose to the surface and were discarded all at once as the prince set his drink down. Nailo returned her easy smile with one of his own, close mouthed yet no less relaxed, a sudden shift from the tension that lined his muscle seconds before. A muscle twitched in Vierna's cheek at the realization, yet no words left her mouth before Nailo spoke.

"Your son saved my friends and I in our time of need, my lady. But I am sure that is not what you would like to hear today, is it?" his hands clasp behind his back gently, his shoulders squared.
"I hope the concept of love and forgiveness has not been lost on the noble houses of Penumbra. If that is the case, however, I would not be opposed to providing you with a demonstration."

A small nod of his head downwards cut short whatever blazing comeback the lady could come up with, a clear sign he was to rid himself of her presence. And no matter the self importance one assigned to themselves, a Royal dismissal is not one easily disobeyed. Out of courtesy, or perhaps a little mischief, he left her with a few words.
"Always a pleasure to converse with you, my lady. But if you'll excuse me, I do intend on making good on my promise to you"

The snake matriarch of Menzo could do little else but gape at the retreating back of the crown prince, who, true to his word, bee lined to a certain green eyed drow only to wrap his arm around the other in a manner the present nobles would later describe as shameless. In his recent absence, Vierna cleared her throat, finishing her glass before striding away in that same, graceful walk; the usually confident line of her shoulders laying fractured under the realization that there was little of the crown prince she could sink her teeth into.
