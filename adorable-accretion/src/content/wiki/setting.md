## Setting 
We are playing an extreme high fantasy setting with magic permeating every nook and cranny of the world. Everybody has some connection to magic and cast cantrips and even level 1 spells by the time they reach adulthood. Learning higher level magic, however, is reserved for the gifted, humanoids that show a special prowess for the arcane. Selection programs search for such gifted candidates that are then trained in the secluded academies of the Dominions. Still magic was utilized in everyday life by basically everybody. Fey and other magical creatures roamed the lands and lived beside the races in harmony. 

Over the years arcane-infused tools have become quite popular, provided by some of the Dominions. Many a craftsman, farmer and hunter relies on these tools. As time passed the reliance on these magical tools grew, however, there was still a (small) part of the population that relied on their inherent magical abilities as they could not afford such tools or simply refused to use them. 

Then one day the wizards and druids flooded to the capital, all talking about a recession of magic. It was said that a great change would happen.

Ever since that day the magic in the lands grew weaker. The tools stopped being effective, inherent magic became harder and more exhausting to use. This time prompted innovation and so came about the technological revolution. At first the machines were powered by arcane cores, but those were soon replaced by steam engines, as the cores became less and less reliable. 

The storms came with the metal servants. At first the storms were only a slight inconvenience, but each storm was stronger than the last, until they were truly devastating. Now towers and storm-shelters cover the lands announcing a coming storm so the citizen can take shelter in time. The fey left or hid, we only know that their numbers started decreasing and their magic grew weaker. 

## Conflict

The upper and middle classes had little problem adapting the metal automatons and reinforcing their homes against the unnatural storms. Jobs are being replaced by sturdy machines that could withstand nature.

The lower classes are struggling, but are starting to band together. Some search for new and other kinds of magic, others see the Dominions as the true enemies.

As this mechanical revolution is just starting and great change is indeed upon the world, we turn to a select few, members of various circles, that all have received an invitation from a prestigious members of one of the leading Dominions.
