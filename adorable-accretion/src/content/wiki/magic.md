The Magic in Abaron has been heavily influenced by the leyline shift.

Magic is everywhere, academies of magic may look to the stars for guidance and power, the druids look toward the nature and there are more sources still.
Magic permeates every part of the world and the world reacts to this. The stars align and are a direct cosmic force of magic, nature forms around leylines and even stores magic itself. Looking to the world for answers is ingrained in the practice of magic and every caster has to find their guidance in the world.

Usually a distinction is made between arcane magic and primordial magic. Arcane magic is often harnessed by Wizards, Bards, Druids and the like, it is drawn from the magic that originates in the leylines and permeates the world. For this the caster draws power directly from their source, may it be the stars or something else. 
Primordial magic is drawn from the primordial gods that govern Abaron or one of their aspects.
