The Session opened with a brief but tense scene between Vhaerun and the blue dragonborn general. Questions are asked, but the answers are not satisfactory. A warning is extended as the dragonborn eludes to some sort of memory related problem for Vhaerun. 

Party discusses the Curse upon Nailos head, then a wind burst lifts the iron-dust and makes the bottom of the Dustwell possible, many things could be seen living or rather spending live down here. Amongst the many creatures and bots were 2 peculiar bots, a many legged drill bot and a hovering bot with a glass head. These two seemed to move as a pair and with purpose. The group eventually followed the bots into the desolate ruins of Ai´ruma before abandoning the pursuit, fearing another combat. 

Choosing to follow the bots at a larger distance the party stumbled upon a large collapsed section of Ai´ruma with the only way forward via large sewer pipes. Upon further inspection and an unfortunate solo trip from Noon, a small combat encounter ensued which was eventually ended with the help of a new arrival, Aaron a Starwielder mage. 

As it turns out Aaron and Reverie share a past and know each other quite well. This prompts Aaron to help the party and guide them through the sewers to his camp. Once there Aaron shows the party the subjects of his current studies, automatons that life in harmony with nature, here in hidden caves below even Ai´ruma. Tense and emotional conversations end this session, as Aaron and Reverie catch up and talk about events past and as Mask talks to Akira about their Mother-Daughter relationship with Noon.
