Lonely. Cruel. Cold. 

The Frostfang mountains had earned their name with no other competition to speak of. Adventurers who were brave enough to climb up rarely came back down and those that did often lost limbs, as they came back home with everfrost plaguing their bodies. No one knows how many lives those frozen peaks have taken.

Located next to Shiverfall in the frozen tundras of Abaron.
