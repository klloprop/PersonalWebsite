A small lumberjack village outside of Raphia. Only one engineer remains here as its sole citizen. The work is completely automated and performed by large machines. 

Agdan Highead, an old dwarf, is the only one that calls this place home. 

> "I'm old, I know my way around repairs and metalwork so they gave me a job at all, but it's no promise. At least this way I can support my family in Raphia. It's just sad that I cant see them a lot lately. "
