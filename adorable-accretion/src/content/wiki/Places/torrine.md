A small lumberjack village outside of Raphia. Only one engineer remains here as its sole citizen. The work is completely automated and performed by large machines. 

Agdan Highead, a old dwarf, is the only one that calls this place home. 

> "Im old, i know my way around repairs and metalwork so they gave me a job at all, but is show no promise. At least this way i can support my family in Raphia. Its just sad that i cant see them a lot lately. "
