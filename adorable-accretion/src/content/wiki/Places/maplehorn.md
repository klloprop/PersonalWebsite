A village completely on wooden stilts that slowly drifts through the swamp on a cyclic path.

As our heroes arrived in Maplehorn all was well, but soon after a mighty necrotic storm hit the village. Many houses were destroyed but thanks to the efforts of our heroes few lives were lost.

Most villagers left Maplehorn after the storm, dooming the village.

A witch by the name of Alecto La Croix once resided here. She even provided boons to the party, but left after Faena, her foster daugther shared some concering information with her.
