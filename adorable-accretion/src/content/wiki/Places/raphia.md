Raphia is build on Ai'rumas ruins, marking the mortals capital in both the Feywild and the Mirrorworld.

Raphia is merely the new age name for the towering structures reaching for the clouds, the gigantic furnaces running all around the clock and the new automaton workforce, slowly driving out the mortals. 

Iron dust permeates this area, which has strong anti magical qualities. 

Fey live under the city.
