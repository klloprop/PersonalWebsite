# TODO
Raphia is build on Ai'rumas ruins, marking the mortals capital in both the Feywild and the Mirrorworld.

Raphia is merely the new age name for the towering structures reaching for the clouds, the gigantic furnaces running all around the clock and the new automaton workforce, slowly driving out the mortals. 

Iron dust permeates this area, which has strong anti magical qualities. 

Fey live under the city. 

- Big golden city, a place of magic and temples and ritual places, once flat, beautiful and serene
- With the technological revolution well underway, this city is primarily governed by the Ivory dominion
- focus on trade and with the technological revolution also birthplace for industry and many innovations
- meeting place for mechanics and inventors
- In time the sacred magical sites will be overshadowed by scaffolding and industry sprining up from the ground rising higher and higher into the sky with the most expensive residences being located at the very top of the developing frontier
	- Piltover/Corusant imagery
	- Heart of the (quickly developing) mechanical world 

- built around a star-disc a powerful ritual site to commune and study the stars
	- as leylines move and the city grows the disc will to be consumed until one day when it can no longer see the sky
	- city mirrors the circular layout/shape of the disc and the old Raphia
### Theme
- They have built to high, long way to fall
- powerful building on the backs of the poor
### Ideology
- "If you work hard enough you can achieve anything"

## Iron Storm
- during rapid early construction a dust and iron storm was kicked up that lasted for days, in this bad visibility and and the particulates in the air caused a lot of injuries and death, the Ivory Dominion did not care about this
- this dust is infused with magical residue 
- This sparked early attempts at revolution, that have yet to come to fruition


# Places
## Sewers
Here reside Deepseadrops and Deepseafloods as well as nature sprouting again on arcane ground, creating its own bioluminescent area. Herein also resides The Circle of Automata.

## Dustwell
The Dustwell reaches from the towers all the way down to the surface of Ai'ruma. This deep chasm has been left open and is used as a dump by everyone. Discarded bots roam here. You can find the Clockwork Cohort, the Collector and the Search engine


## Ai´ruma
The old fey name for Raphia, now commonly used for the arcane ruins that lay dormant and hidden below the golden towers. 
### Vortex Spa

has a connection to the old sewers underneath Raphia


After Aliana sparked the flame of ambition Ai´Ruma grew into the unfathomable. A paradise of magic and wonder. Aliana herself, the only one to ever solve the issue of mortality led her people into a golden age, revered as a god. 

Three giant towers give the city a distinct skyline, channeling the three leylines that meet here into a masterpiece of magic: the Stardisk. The Emerald grove burned long ago, but years work differently for the immortal fey, their retaliation soon to arrive here. The story would play out as the eldritch fey are spotted and Ai'ruma mounts its last defense, the last spells are cast and the Stardisk eventually cracks. Humanities first Calamity. (edited)
