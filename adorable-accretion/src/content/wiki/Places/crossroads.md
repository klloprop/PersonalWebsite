The central city that connects the overworld, umbra and what lies below.
A travele and trading hub. Individuals from all over Abaron go here to sell the most exotic things. 

A hanging city made of stone, wood, rock and crystal, suspened on giant metal chains from the ceiling.
The city itself is build in a large opening that reaches from the surface all the way into the upper layers of the caverns. 
The only easily accessible and safe route to travel to and from the underground.

Three main gates secure the borders of the city, the Earthen gate at the surface, the Devils gate, leading to the caverns and the Umbran gate leading to the rest of the underground.


## Currently

The Crossroads are occupied by the ivory dominion. Trade has died down with the war making the streets unsafe to travel and restricting funds from potential buyers. Around a third of the crossroads buildings stand empty as travelers abandon their stores there to find safer buisness deeper in the ivory dominion.
