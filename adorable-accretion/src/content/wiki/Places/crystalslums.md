The crystal slums of the Ramshorn Area. The slums are a byproduct of the ivory dominion abandoning the mining operations in the greater Ramshorn area.

The crystal slums are located wherever the mining operation has left homes unoccupied. Ex-miners afflicted by the curse bear the mark of the crystals on their skin. A symptom of the dominions influence resulting in poverty and suffering.
