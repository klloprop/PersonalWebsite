The highest mountains in the world reside here on a plateau above the clouds.

People use specialised windmills for all sorts of things, they use the power of the wind to power their everyday life. 

The landscape is filled with sparse greenery and beautiful pink blossom trees that fill the valleys every summer with the pink petals. Locals refer to the clouds of petals drifting down the mountainsides on mellow wind currents as pink rivers. 

On the highest point in the plateu, surrounded by hostile frozen winds a city lies, Lyrengorn. The frozen forests and landscapes may incur the image of a place frozen in time in a brave travelers mind.
Lyrengorn sits inside a caldera of a inactive volcano, shielded by two large mountain like structures. This place is called "Split mountain". This environment and the residual heat in the mountain allows a small and unique civilisation to reside here, closest to the stars.
