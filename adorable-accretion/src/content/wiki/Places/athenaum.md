Most notably, Athenaum is build in the space where the Feywild and the Mirrorworld touch. The mirrorplane, as it is called by scholars, roughly sits in the center of the city, halve of it residing in the feywild and the other halve located in the mirrorworld.
Since the Mirrorworld has been first created, has been seen as a place of exchange and peace, a place where fey and mortals meet and share. 

The city is governed by a shared council of fey and mortal.

### Summit Athenaeum
Athenaum once contained the biggest library in all the lands called **Summit Athenaeum**, but that same library burned down as the leylines shited. This catastrophy resulted in the gap between richer folk and poorer folk widening. Slowing the exchange of knowledge and magic.

The library was once believed to be sentient, the very wood of the building being a leftover from the dryads of old. Today this is remembered as a folk tale that likely wasn't true. 

### Seriara Academy
Athenaum further harbour the most prestigious and powerful Academy of the Starwielder dominion, the **Seriara** Academy, in its mirrorworld halve.
Reverie eventually earned the title of mage here.

### Aesthetic  

Fey architecture and mortal architecture meet here. Extravagant ivory walls build for eternity meet practical wooden workshops. The city is marked by many large gardens, lakes and rivers that run deep into the outskirts of the city.
