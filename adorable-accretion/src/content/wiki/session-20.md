(without mik and kenny)

The party has arrived in Aneridge as three creatures fly over their heads in formation. Noon could determine that it is the Elder Knight, Lionell, on his silver dragon companion Volnescend, Hallorn the Faceless on his steed Sir Scrungle and Guldor, on a normal griffin. It is werid that Guldor usually only rides on Lanost, their Goldwing partner, but now is seen on a griffin.

Hallorn breaks from the formation to land in the inner ring. As the party turns a corner it turns out that the large black leathery beast and the Faceless knight are standing not far from them. Hallorn is swept up in conversation with a blonde man of the ivory dominion, both are surrounded by multiple Brightstriders. Sir Scrungle smells the strong illusion magic that is placed on the party, reacting strongly. Realising the potential dangers of this situation the party make a run for a nearby alleyway, however before they can make it to safety Nailo seems to have an involuntary reaction spewing (like a squid) thin metal flakes from his tail. A trained eye could determine that this metal is magnesium. 

In the alleyway a door suddenly opens and a baker beckons the party inside. It turns out that the bakery, the Bun Boutique (yes it has bread-bunny designs), is owned by Sasha and his husband Wu, both are opposed to the Brightstriders and the dominions in general. The party learns that the Brightstriders have been conducting raids in the area, lead by none other than Hallorn, on the search for some sort of spy-ring. Eventually, after most of the commotion outside has died down, the party heads to a random corner-store, with a special baked good, which is supposed to act as a sort of password for the owner. The Antique store is run by a young elf woman, who lazily flips through some book as the party arrives. The young elf is swiftly sent off as the old lady emerges from some backroom. A swift distrustful conversation later, the eyes of the old human starts to glow blue-ish, unbeknownst to the party, seeing through their illusions. A hand raises and a quick rune is drawn in the air as all of the illusions in the room are dispelled. The party finds themselves standing in front of Xia Lueur, Nailos mother and Queen of Penumbra. The red tiefling quickly embraces her son and and quick introductions are made.

Xia leads the party through a small hidden portal in the basement, which connects to a apartment somewhere else in the city. Stood around a table and peering at a map are Aeron, and Astrea, both younger siblings of Nailo, and Nailos former body guard now commander of the Shadowguard, Kyordal Chaulssinn. The party learns that someone else is smuggling goods through Aneridge and this has been noticed by the Brightstriders. Someone must have tipped the knights of to some spy ring, and now they are conducting raids to search for their little group. Their goal has only been the collection of information, nothing more. 

The sessions ends as the party is in the apartment and Nailo has received a orb of scrying, allowing him to talk safely to his dads.
