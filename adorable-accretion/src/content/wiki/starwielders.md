The Starwielders are one of the most powerful Dominions in Abaron. Known for their mastery over arcane forces, they have taken it upon themselves to study and document the natural phenomena of the world.

## The Storm-Callers

One of the Starwielders' most notable contributions is the network of storm-caller towers. As [[storms]] grew more dangerous, they built towers equipped with gigantic horns to warn citizens of approaching danger.

## Organization

The Starwielders maintain strict hierarchies and are known for their scholarly pursuits. Their libraries contain vast amounts of knowledge about the world of Abaron.
