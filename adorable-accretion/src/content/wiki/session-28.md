The party starts, having just stepped foot into the caverns, featureless grey caves leading slowly underground. 
After hours of travel the slope of their current passage increases until it fades into stairs spiraling down on the outside of tower.
At the foot of the tower they find a path that leads inside, to, what seems to be, a resting site. Trusting this welcome find the party decides to long rest here.

During the rest small spirits watch over them...

Refreshed the group continues their exploration, heading deeper into the caves. They come upon large caves filled with vegetation, fireflies and a large electrical lizard. Barely managing to find cover from a underground storm the party rests for a moment

It appears  the Game master has died (/j) the session has been stopped.
