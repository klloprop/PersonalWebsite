(started strong, but lost Mik, Kenny and Ghost)

Freshly arrived in Raphia, the party sought out masks and heavy cloaks to protect them against the ever present iron dust filling up the lower layers of Raphia. Making their way into the city they meet the heat  of the giant furnaces that supply the insatiable city with metal. In the city they are introduced to the humble abode of Arkus and Arkus sick, possibly dying, brother Tanyll. They learn that the two people they are looking for, an alchemist and an artificer, have been disrupting the construction efforts of the golden towers. Looking into the same incident they discover that a special compound was used to effectively burn through thick metal (in this case the engine core of automatons). With Gawaines expertise, they learn that this compound is partly created from a common catalyst and the concentrated extract of a special mushrooms that usually grows around places of fey magic.

Knowing only one place magical enough in Raphia, the party decides to head to Ai´ruma, the buried city that once, in more magical times, was known as Raphia. To get to the arcane ruins they would need to brave the Dustwell, a iron dust filled hole, purposefully left to easily dispose of junk.
