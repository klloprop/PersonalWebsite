The party was faced with a enraged and revenge-driven archfey that wants to invade Raphia and eliminate the careless progress that currently pervades the lands. The party, not ready to ally themselves with Moffolistril, try to talk their way out but only anger the fey more, until eventually their partner, Vanulloth steps in and helps to negotiate a peaceful resolution. 

Vanulloth leads the party to a secret transport way that run throughout the underground, a large underground river, inhabited by kelpie. Aaron says his goodbyes to Reverie and the party heads upwards again to meet with the artificer and alchemist duo the party had been searching for. The kelpies take them into the Vortex Spa, from which they can easily find their mark.

The session ends with a tense conversation between Carmelio and Lucius about their situation and that Carmelios situation might be precedented after all, especially for the first dryads.
