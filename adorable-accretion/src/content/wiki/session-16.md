(Full party)

We start with an cold open, showing an interaction between Merith and Faena in a warehouse filled with a metal legion. After a short conversation Faena receives a tuft of Masks fur and is sent to hunt down the group.

Gawaine had his hearing with the witch and appeased her with kindly acts. The party rests at the Witches hotel, which overnight changes to more closely accommodate the individual people resting in their rooms. All throughout the night and into the next morning a storm rages, that come next morning, escalates from a level 1 storm to a level 2 storm. 

Our heroes are faced with a choice, either stay in their safe hotel and wait out the storm or venture outside into the dangerous weather and try to help the villagers, as many houses collapse and are destroyed by the storms influence. Eventually the party gathers in the town hall, the sturdiest building in Maplehorn. There with the rest of the village, they have to form a plan to escape this perilous situation. 

Meanwhile Sasha recognizes the arcana and the potential of this storm. It is a storm of undeath. With some protective wards from his friends he ventures outside in an attempt to catch green lightning in a bottle. After the initial harrowing attempt failed and left Sasha half dead in the mud, he decides to try again, this time successfully catching an 'Essence of Undeath'.
