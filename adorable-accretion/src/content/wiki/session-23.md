(With Everyone)

Everyone returns to the safehouse to welcome back Akira, Wes and Arneal.

Arneal reveals to everyone that Noon is responsible for the Aneridge Scourge, and has directly contributed to the death of over 25000 citizen.
This reveal causes Noon to recede in herself and have something akin to a panic attack. She ends up walking through the streets, an growing blizzard accompanying her. Mask tries to stop her but their attempts ultimately fail. Just as Mask storms back into the room to ask the others for help Reverie awakens from her slumber. Everybody except for Reverie, Arneal and Nailos Family leave to help Noon, who is already being surrounded by Brightstriders. 

Pandora and Vesper are also drawn to the giant commotion in town. 

The party manages with combined efforts to help Akira grab Pandora and get her to relative safety before anything horrible can happen. In the joint escape from the group as the alarm bells are rung, small, still mostly wooden, Arkus is snatched up by a griffin.
