## Leyline Shift
The event that started the age of "Receding Magic" (It is sometimes called this, scholars and farmers, students and traders all use different language for this) is undeniably the leyline shift. The magic stopped flowing as freely through the lands and the places of magical potency shifted, if not vanished all together. 

As a result of this magic grew weaker and casting became more cumbersome and exhausting. People could no longer rely on magic alone to manage their daily lives.

From a gameplay perspective you can think of this that basically everybody had like 2 or three times more spellslots than we have right now according to the ruleset. Before the leylineshift a level 20 wizard could maybe go around and have 3 or more level 9 spellslots available while now you have 1 max.
