(Without Mik)

The party is reeling from the events of the last session. The evening is spent recouperating and spending time together. 

We start with Akira and Noon in a heartbreaking scene, where they talk about Akiras husband and the reunions in the rest of the group.

Later in the evening the group relocates to a hotel to avoid the domions, as Arkus leaves behind a letter to his brother.
Conversations ensue about the nature of Reveries relationship with Aaron and a few realisations follow.

Further Nailo awaits the return of Vhaerun to have a short but painful conversation about a forgotten earring and about their current relationship.

The session ends with the group meeting their illustrious contact in the emerald cove, this contact turns out to be a gigantic owlin named Arnael Gleamlight.
