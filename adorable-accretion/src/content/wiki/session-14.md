(Without Kenny and Mik)

The session starts with everyone arriving at the place to meet with Arneal. Reverie has many flowers in her hair and has entered her summer season, evident on most of her body. Mask arrives sheepishly hiding behind carmelio as they are wearing a new dress. In addition to that Ivvory arrives with Arkus in tow. 

After a short moment the party gets into a large carriage (dont ask) and they make off to some witch to procure curse wards going forward to move against the dominions.

On their travels they are intercepted by a golden knight and a small ensemble of primordial mages and paladins. Pascal-Phillipe seeks answers regarding a sword artifact, specifically he seeks Akria for some yet unkown reason. The situation quickly escalates as he recognize Noon, formerly known as Summer Afternoon, and demands her head.

The ensuing fight is brutal and sees the heroes scattered. After a few turns Carmelio receives a strong hit from Pascal-Phillipe and goes down, his core overloading and exploding, injuring many.

Using this moment and a a good use of Noons Everfrost touch they manage to break the concentration of Pascal-Phillipes Banishment spell, allowing Arneal in his monstrous form to finally help the party. Soon after they manage to retrieve the wounded and flee from the Brightstriders for a moment, Arneal soon turning back to buy the group more time.

Carmelio was destroyed, many of his core components still intact. Roughly half of the party was unconscious due to their wounds.

After a tense moment and a use of the sea of knowledge from Reverie, she convinces Arkus to help her revive Carmelio, but not without a compromise. Arkus installs a failsafe that, should Carmelio ever be strongly damaged again and the core energy surge, redirect all that energy into his heart, vaporizing him immediately.

The session ended as Carmelio opens his eyes once more.

Between this and the next session the events of the 'Cracks through the Mask' and 'The Aftermath' role play thread take place.
