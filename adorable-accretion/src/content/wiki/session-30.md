The session begins with a cold open, showing Aaron being rescued from the hostile fey forests. He stands in a abode, a shapeshifter in front of him, offering him shelter and food. Eventually Aaron eats, from the offerings and the being reveals that it holds no name, but is often called "king".

The party is still locked in combat with Troglodytes. Eventually the group flees after striking down many of the blind cavern dwellers. Leaving the crystal caves, the hunting grounds of the troglodytes, the heroes are met with the heat of the hells. Arriving the in the enormous fiery cave, they quickly meet a devil. Kraz. To everyone's surprise this devil does not seem hostile, or very devilish at that. This particular individual was napping on a stone and is even offering to guide the party to the closest metropolis, the city of Obsidian. 

Once in Obsidian the party is met with many curious glances, apparently mortals are indeed a rare sight down here. Kraz, while being a very loud individual, ends up being very useful, introducing the party to Naphim, the shy, protector of the city of Obsidian. Naphim in turn introduces the party to Zeb the king of the hells and at the end of the session the group finds themselves in the office of a obsidian tower, overlooking the black glass cityscape.
