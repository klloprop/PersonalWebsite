Finishing their rest in a old workshop, Mask checks in on Akira. Akira is torn and distraught, being forced to leave her husband, Wes, behind once more. 
Mask tries their best to cheer the cervitaur up but ultimately has little success. Looking for support Mask turns to Noon, who is in the process of designing a board game, apparently a well tested method to help clear Akiras mind. Mask is eventually invited for some quality "family time", as Noon put it and the trio continues to design a board game. 

Arkus prepares some materials for a future automaton.

After the storm has passed the group continues down the large green cave, quickly finding a altar to Nocros. While the primordial magic is overwhelming and many offerings are scattered around the group moves on after a small investigation. The party does learn that Indigo Aurora, the same stone used in automatons, are called soul crystals as they appear after somebody has departed. The blue soul crystals in question still contain a soul or at least some part of it, while the green soul crystals are the remnant of a soul having departed to some other place.

Continuing on, the group soon starts noticing that every few hours the surrounding are repeating themselves, the cave seems to have trapped them in a spatial loop. Finding the original altar again Noon manages to manifest her connection to Nocros and open a path. The street opens up revealing a passage below. This starts a chain reaction and all nearby buildings, structures and rocks start sinking slowly. 

Further ahead the heroes stumble into a cavern filled with countless soul crystals and are ambushed by blind intelligent underground dwellers. Troglodytes. 

The battle continues as the session ends.
