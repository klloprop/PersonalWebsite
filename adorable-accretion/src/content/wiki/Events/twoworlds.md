The fey, lead by the Nameless King, create the Mirrorworld. Much like the name implies the Mirrorworld started as a reflection of the Feywild but soon got formed by mortals into its own place.

Students of the planes stipulate that the planes in fact lay right next to each other, both touching, what the scholars call, 'the Mirrorplane'.

This event started the mortals date system. Generally in Abarons Mirrorworld ac refers to "After Creation of the Mirrorworld" and bc refers to "before the creation of the Mirrorworld".

Nature vs Nurture depicts the events of Alianas life leading up to this moment.
