As an introduction to this session we explore two more perspectives leading up to the brief death of Mask. We see Vhaerun, struggling with the 'forget-me-not' curse, trying to hold onto every memory he had of nailo, and Guldor, golden knight and former mentor of Noon intervening in the fight and facing 'The Ivory Might' himself. 

The crew at Arkus abode decided to wait until the weird magical phenomenon, unbeknownst to them caused by Carmelio, has settled. Afterwards Sasha and Noon make their way to the outskirts of the city, where the iron dust is not ever present so the wish of the Sunchaser Moth Gawaine can be fullfilled. On the way Noon overhears guards talking about the commotion and the center of the magical anomaly, causing Noon to head back towards the center of Raphia. Sasha and Gawaine part ways and Gawaine gifts the Alchemists Gamut to Sasha as a show of gratitude. 

In front of the Vortex Spa the rest of the party is arrested by Finkas Leafpot, but Guldur steps in taking custody of the group, saving them momentarily from the Ivory Dominions grasp. They are being escorted to the Brightstriders HQ in Raphia and put into a holding cell. The Ivory Dominion is closing in and is demanding of the Brightstriders to hand over the party. Meanwhile the group is reflecting about what happened, Reverie taps into a new-found power and Guldor asks the group about Noons well being. 

Soon after Vhaerun arrives and with the use of some magical tricks tries to bust the party out of their predicament. Almost at the same time Noon arrives with similar goals, but is confronted by Guldor before reaching their friends. 

An emotional scene between Guldor and Noon ensues and Guldor eventually lets them go.
The party escapes and regroups at Arkus home. 

They are now wanted and known by the dominions. They have learned that the 'Ivory Might' is hunting them, a fighter of modern legend. They have learned of curses, Dominion plans and had reunions. 

For now they can rest and recover.

Still the next day awaits. This is story is far from over.
