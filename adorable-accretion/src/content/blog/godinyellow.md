___
I don't remember how I got here. The streets are empty, truly and absolutely empty. 
I find myself standing on the sidewalk between skyscrapers, in fact, all i can see are skyscrapers. Grey monoliths as far as the eye can see.
I don't recognize anything here. The buildings look more like the idea of buildings, their facades are featureless, only windows break up the concrete monotony. There are no street signs or any signs for that matter. All of it looks the same. 
A concrete maze. 
The streets follow this trend too. Everything is organized in a perfect grid, the same bland traffic lights at every intersection, slowly blinking yellow. 

Not a single car or piece of trash breaks up the monotony.

I start wandering aimlessly. This cannot be all there is, right? There has to be *something* out there. It's very quiet. Each footstep echos like a gunshot along the streets, but there is something else too. 
Crackling. 
Not louder than a whipser. 

It takes me a moment to find its source, the windows. From a distance they look normal, the interiors beyond are dark and generically decorated. Stepping closer, the cracks reveal themselves. Every single window is shattered, but somehow every single window remains in its place, the small pieces of glass stacked on top of each other like a crazy game of jenga.

What could have done this? Maybe a distant explosion? A earthquake? The glass does not look like it shattered from a object impacting it, it more so... just shattered. Sometimes it spiders out from a corner, sometimes there doesn't seem to be a origin. The shattered windows finally gave me a point of reference in this otherwise featureless maze. If I remember the intricate patterns i could surely use them as markers. Maybe i could keep myself from getting lost.

I start looking at the last window on the left hand side before entering a intersection, to see if there is a pattern or any noticeable differences between the windows, but they all seem random. Some are shattered to the point of almost turning white, others are barely damaged. I start to only move forward or turn left, to reduce my chances of accidentally getting turned around. 

It doesn't take long until the patterns speak to me. 
They tell a story of a world im not ready to hear.
When I close my eyes I see the patterns. 

At least this should make it easier to compare patterns... 

It occurs to me that I could call out. That maybe someone would answer, but something tells me that nobody would hear me. That I am alone.

In the beginning I stayed on the sidewalk, force of habit i guess, but soon I start walking in the middle of the street and a sense of... freedom overcame me. 
Its a weird feeling, to walk in the street. It takes a while before the urge to climb into the safety of the sidewalk disappears. 
I wonder why im here. Am I dreaming? Am I dead? Is this punishment or just another force of nature?  

The sky is overcast and grey, giving me little information about the time of day. I wonder if night will fall. It should right? Maybe it won't. One way or another I do not look forward to sleep on concrete.
___
I come across a particularly shattered window. The individual glass piece are at various angles, creating a somewhat mesmerizing visual. I find myself staring for a bit, allowing my burning legs a short break. 
My eyes drift to a splash of color, a spot of yellow in the grey landscape. It takes me a moment to understand what I am looking at, but then i recognize the shape. Humanoid, a person. My breaths turn shallow and my hairs stand on end. I turn, pushing my back against a concrete wall as I scan my surroundings. 
Nothing. 

Carefully, I turn back to the reflection. It is still there. I turn around to check the spot again. Nothing.
Something tells me it is not human. Sure, it looks human, but it also doesn't. That small part of my brain, that part beyond rationale is screaming that it is *weird*. I force myself closer to the reflection, trying to make out more details but it is too far away. It seems to just stand there, not moving, clad in something yellow, maybe a raincoat? I move to another window and sure enough after enough searching I find it here too. The same yellow coat. 
It is moving with me.

... or am I moving with it? 

The initial panic dissipates slowly, still, a uneasy feeling remains. I wonder if there are more things watching me from afar, from the reflections or maybe from some dark windows higher up. I start moving on the sidewalks again, staying close to a building to escape at least some of the windows sight lines. It follows me. Is it a predator? A demon? 

After a while a thought occurs to me. Alien, not my own. 

I think its a **god**. 

Is it here to enact punishment or offer aid? To judge or to observe? It takes me a few attempts to speak. It feels sacrilegious and wrong to disturb the crackle.
 
"Hello?" I call out.

"I can see you." I yell. 

"You are wearing a yellow coat!" No response. Nothing. I try a few doors to see if i can find a place to hide and rest in the buildings, away from the glass, but every single one is locked. I don't think i could break down a door and the idea of breaking a window feels... *very wrong*.

With no other option I continue onward. Every maze has a exit... right?
___
Soon the skyscrapers give way to brown industrial buildings, the red bricks provide a very welcome change of scenery and a splash of color. Did the world change because I called out? Did **god** do this?

The buildings are varied now, but they still feel soulless. More like the idea of industry, of large halls, placed randomly and chimneys not even originating from production floors. 

A place without substance.
Here too, there is not a single sign advertising a companies name.

I check my reflection in a empty guard both. It gets easier to spot the yellow. I feel that my eyes are cracking. 

I start exploring again. I walk down random streets now, turning whatever way feels right. I start looking for that yellow glint in every reflection. Somehow I think it gives me comfort? At least it tells me im not alone.

I start talking to it. I ask if it is god. I tell it how absurd all of this is and that I want to go home. Whatever "home" means. 
I don't remember. 

I ask what it does, what it can do. Much like a prayer, there is no answer. At least not one provided with words. I turn a corner and spot a green shape. It looks like a tank. A war machine. I walk down that street towards the dormant thing. I should be mortified facing down a machine like this, but im not sure it matters here. Metal doesn't crack like glass.

Climbing onto the turret, it takes me a moment to figure out how to open the latch. The entire thing looks new, no sign of combat, no track marks on the concrete below. Out of place. Like me. 

I feel sympathy for it.
I wonder if it too will crack.

The inside is claustrophobic. I attempt to play around with the controls but I have no idea what im doing. I can't get the engine to start and eventually I give up. I do find some sort of gun, a rifle. I probably shouldn't take it. It will only weigh me down. I consider if I could use the gunpowder from the cartridges to start a fire, but i haven't seen anything else that could burn...

Also im not really cold... or warm.

At last I leave the green giant to rest. A sling is fitted on the rifle. Carrying it over a shoulder should not be too bad right? It makes me feel safer.

There is less glass here, the red bricks choke almost every wall, only small openings left for windows. I find myself seeking out the shattered bits and pieces, searching for god in every reflection. Whenever i spot that splash of yellow, my mind calms.
The cracking feels louder now.

The abandoned industry feels safe. Those empty buildings come with the promise that there once were people here, but are no longer. Familiar and empty. 

Well, free until you come upon a locked door and as far as I can tell they all are. I wonder if I could shoot it open, like in the movies. But the peace of this place seems complete and besides I would only find a way to hurt myself. 

My feet ache, but there is no point in stopping. Eventually the red brick fades behind me as family homes and farmsteads appear in front of me. I talk to god as I walk. Just talking feels good. With trees on the horizon I notice that there is no wind. None at all. 
The world is frozen solid. 

My stomach churns and rumbles. 
I hear the crackling where ever I go. 
I feel like the day should be coming to an end, but the sky gives no indication of any time passing. The grey wall above looms endlessly. The buildings are getting sparse now. Im leaving the city behind. That fact makes me feel uneasy. What am I even looking for?
___
Maybe god heard me, saw my suffering. I see a supermarket up ahead. The first of its kind. The large windows allow me a glance inside. It is stocked. I see food, even fresh bread and fruit. The doors wont open for me. I walk around the premises a few times, testing every door and opening, but I don't find a way inside. 

My mind is slipping away from me, the hunger is now overpowering any other thought. The solution is simple, really. I just need to help that glass along its way. Shatter it completely. I hold the gun up high like a bat, ready to swing at one of the large windows. 
It feels wrong. 
Sacrilegious. 
I speak a prayer, asking for forgiveness.

Then I swing. 

I make contact and the entire world erupts. Every shattered pane of glass explodes at the same time. The gun swings through the window with little resistance. The world is filled with enormous sound for a few long moments. My ears ring and I stare at the shards on the ground. There is no food inside. 
It was only a reflection. 
A trap.
I look around, everywhere the ground is covered in tiny glistening shards, the window sockets now empty. 
What have I done?

I feel my eyes shatter too. 
I have shattered.

I kneel, picking up the shards and turning them in my hand.
Im looking for its reflection. For the yellow god.
I already know I won't find it. 
Its gone.
It *was* a prisoner, just like me.

I grab the gun tight and pray it is loaded. 

In the shattered remains I see clearly.
I am not alone anymore.

It must be hungry.
