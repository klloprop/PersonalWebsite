"Don't look now, I think we made the sun cry"

"There is a story that every child here is told: The story of a great queen, queen Lydia. The queen was beloved by her subjects and brought prosperity and innovation to her lands. People from all over started travelling to this kingdom in search of that same prosperity. Over time this sparked many conflicts, other kingdoms did not like their subjects leaving and the progressive ideas of queen Lydia. 

Sadly history is not kind to good people. So the kingdoms banded together to wage war against ours. Our armies were greatly outnumbered and there was no chance of victory. Lydia knew that, she also knew that a prolonged war would only lead to more suffering.

However, the queen wasn't just a beloved ruler, she was aa talented namescribe of highest rank. When she learned of the enemy armies closing in, she sent her namescribes into her kingdom to ask the people to pray in her name, as the queen herself prepared to meet the enemy on the battlefield. Only, you see, the queen did not just ask the people to pray, she asked them to pray to her true name. Her namescribes would teach her true name to anyone who would ask."

"But why her true name, mama?"

"Every language holds power. The old languages hold the most, and our names in those tongues, our true names do too. If I say your name now Maria, you might feel a small tinge or even a small discomfort. This is because simply by saying your name energy from me flows to you. This is a very very small amount of energy of course. If I now gathered 10 people to say your name together, you might find yourself to smile for no reason, if I gathered 100 people, you might feel warm on the inside. If I would instead use your true name the energy could flow unrestricted from me to you and the effect would be much greater.

That day thousands upon thousands of people prayed to her true name. Not just a simple mention, but entire paragraphs dedicated to her. 

She was a daughter of a smith and her true name was one of fire. 

That day queen Lydia razed the enemy armies to the ground in a firestorm that is said to have reached beyond the heavens and burned as bright as the sun. 
As the sky was ablaze and fire rained to the earth it must have looked like the sky descended. The firestorm lasted for hours and could be seen from very, very far away. 

We remember that day, as the **day of the crying sun**.

This is the greatest feat of magic to ever be recorded in our history."

"So she won and defeated the evil armies and saved her lands!"

"That is sadly not the end of the story, history isn't kind to good people. When queen Lydia returned from the battle her skin was smoldering with burning hot embers that could not be extinguished, her eyes had been replaced with red rubies that contained a raging fire and her skin was covered in obsidian shards, almost like scales.

Hours later she fell violently ill and even died before sunset. Enemy spies learned of her true name and their namescribes made short work of her. Lydia knew of the sacrifices that she was making.

Thanks to her our armies could stand against the remaining attackers. 

This kingdom only exists today because of her. This is why our family name is 'sun'."

Authors note: It is rumored that queen Lydia incinerated after she perished. Some speculate that she was born anew, as a higher form, a phoenix. We only know that there are no remains in her grave, with the exception of a thin lining of ash.
