I check the clock on the tiled wall, the exam finished 30 minutes ago, she should be here soon. 

Ana has been so nervous for her witch's exam, which in turn means I could barely sleep or do much of anything today. In the end all I did, and still do, is sit in our little kitchen dressed in comfortable pajamas, waiting for her. 
___
The sunrays piercing through the window are already turning orange as I hear footsteps flying up the stairs outside.

*She is excited. Good. That means she probably passed*. 

I smile to myself as I get up from the rickety chair, just in time for the door to fly open and Ana jumping into my arms, hugging me. 
"Hi" I giggle, already infected by her bubbly energy. I gently brush her large witches hat back so our eyes can meet. 
With a big smile she looks up at me and giddily says, "Don't you want to ask me how it went?"
I try to conjure up a nonchalant tone, "How what went? Was there something important today?", but a smug smile is betraying my facade. 
"You- Ah, I guess its just a normal day, nothing to report after all" Ana pouts, before sticking her tongue out at me, but her own facade quickly crumbles into a small smile. 
I raise my hands in defeat with a chuckle, "Ooookay, okay, how did it go?"
Her smile grows bright once more and she jumps, "I PASSED!! I FINALLY EARNED MY OWN BROOM." then her hands fold neatly behind her back and in a quiet tone she adds, "Im a proper witch now!" 
"YOU DID!" I exclaim, before cupping her face with both of my hands "To me you always were". From there my arms easily wrap around her, pulling her into a tight hug.
The embrace lasts blissfully long.
As we finally pull apart a silence has fallen between us and my arms naturally come to rest around her shoulders, fingers interlaced behind her neck, hers land on my hips. 

Our eyes won't meet in these few nervous, but heavenly moments. 

"We should celebrate!!!" Ana exclaims, jumping out of the embrace, "And I know just what to do!" 
Before I can process much of what just happened I am grabbed by the arm and dragged upstairs out onto our balcony. 
"Ana, what are you planning?" I ask, cautiously as the pleasantly cool evening air brushes against my exposed ankles.
"We are going to fly together"
"Haven't you flown enough today already?"
She turns to me with a mellow expression, "Yeah, but this is my first real flight and besides..." she pouts , as her gaze wanders to our feet, "I wanna share that with you."

"But I'm in my paja-"
"Its warm! You'll be fine." she protests, while already pulling her broom outside of her witches hat "And no one will see us anyway."
Before I can object any further Ana already has the broom between her legs, looking at me expectantly. 
"Fiiiiiine." I sigh theatrically, still, a smile tugs at my lips. The prospect of clinging onto her and only her is too good to pass up.

I settle behind her on the thin piece of wood, my hands naturally wrapping around her waist. I look behind me wondering if the base of the broom could keep me from sliding off... It takes me a while, caught up in my own thoughts, to notice that nothing is happening. 
My gaze travels back to Ana, her head is slightly rotated towards me, her lips are parted, as if to speak. 
"Everything okay?" I probe carefully.
"Yeah" she eventually says and turns her attention forward again, "Here goes nothing". Just like that her tone is normal again. *That's new*.

We push off the tiled balcony, quickly soaring past the neighbors window and then past the chimneys. The first airborne moments are unsteady and I fear the broom will shoot out underneath us, but then it stabilizes at last. We soar higher and higher, the houses and streets shrinking beneath us until only a sea of lights remains. 
Suddenly its easy to imagine the humans as ants crawling between stars. 

Eventually our ascend slows. The lake reflects the city lights, making it seem like the city extends far out onto the water, shimmering in many hues of red and orange. Beyond the city and the lake lay dark mountains silhouetted by a quickly darkening blue sky. 
A flock of geese joins us, allowing us into their formation. They honk delightfully, as we soar through the sky together. 
My eyes are glued on a nearby goose as I notice the broom gently gliding closer to that feathered individual. Before I can protests Ana takes one of my hands wrapped around her waist and guides it over to the white feathers. They are soft, and the goose is surprisingly calm about this entire interaction. I giggle as my hand carefully brushes against the feathers. 

Eventually the geese start to descend again, ending our small journey together. I wave at them as they grow tiny in the distance, leaving me and Ana alone once more.

The wind rushes past my ears. The world slowly moves below me. My hands wrapped around her.
It's peaceful, it's perfect.

My gaze travels between Ana and the world for a while, observing all the beauty both have to offer.  Eventually my eyes settle on the witch in front of me. Her large hat is fluttering in the wind, her clothes pressed down between us. 
Despite the cool evening air, I feel warm. 
I push first my nose, then my ear in between her shoulder blades, hugging her tighter. We remain like that. My world has shrunken to encompass only her.

I see other witches fly across the evening sky, on their way home from their various occupations. I also notice how red Anas pointed ears have grown. *Probably just from the wind right?*

I sense her muscles tense before she moves, she hesitates a few times, before finally turning her head towards me. I see her lips form words, just a few, not many, but the wind drowns out any sound.
"WHAT?" I try to fight against the wind.
I think I see her smile? After another moment she turns forward again and starts turning us around. 

Maybe that's what she wanted to tell me. That we will be turning back home.
The flight back is equally stunning, but I have had my fill of the worlds beauty, so I keep my head pressed to her back, drinking in more of her. I'm never tired of her.

I feel us start descend, and before too long I see our balcony growing larger below us. 
Our feet gently find the railing and with a little hop we land back on steady ground. 
I slowly let go of her, stretching my limbs. It is suddenly very quiet without the wind, a nightingale is singing nearby.  

Our eyes meet. Her ears are still red. 
She glances at the ground, opening her mouth to speak but, no sound would escape. 
"That was fun." I offer, "I must say I am jealous of the witches that get to see that kind of view all the time." I say, but I'm not speaking of the town, the geese or the sea. 
She chuckles awkwardly, "Yeah... we can go anywhere now, yknow. We could explore the old castle ruins, or or or I could bring you to the witch covens spring fires!" her eyes alight with her usual spark before dimming again.

 "Did you hear what I said? During the flight." she mumbles.
"No." I sense a nervous hurricane building in my stomach,  "but, I am hearing you now"
"Its more difficult if I know you can hear me." she chuckles, then our eyes meet. 
I can see tears in the corners of her eyes and that same redness, all over her face.
"Oh, Ana" I speak in the softest voice I can muster, "I think I have a idea what's on your mind" I eventually manage to say. I don't reach for her hand, that feels like too much. Instead my arms wrap around my own elbows. 

"Its scary." Ana eventually notes. 
"Yeah..." 
"So?" Ana says, her voice is shaky and small tears run down her cheeks by now, "You have to say it! What you are thinking."
I can't help but chuckle at the messy display in front of me, and as I speak my own voice trembles with the weight of the situation, "Well, I thought I was getting to close, invading your space..." I search her eyes, "But something tells me you don't mind."
"Of course not!" she exclaims, "I wouldn't mind if we were close like that more often."
"Okay."
"Okay."

"....Can I hold you hand?"
