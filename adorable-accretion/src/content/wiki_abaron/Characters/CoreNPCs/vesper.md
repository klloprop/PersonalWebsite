**Pronouns:** She/Her

Primordial Mage to Yatamanaa, Aspect of Ades and part of the Brightstriders as a Cleric.

- goes around the community and helps them however she can
- generally liked and well known person
- however it is known that their help can cause additional troubles, if something is given something is taken
- grants wishes in the scope of her abilities

Is very close with Pandora

## Shapeshifting
- only fulfills wishes/casts magic in private
- when the primoridal arcana flows through her she shapeshifts
- she gains 2 more arms
- potentially gains wings
