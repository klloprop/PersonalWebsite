**Pronouns:** He/She/They/It

A mysterious figure only mentioned for now in the pages of Ouiki.
A First Fey never appearing in the same form twice. True to their name they rarely engage and simply observe the world around them.

Aliana's lover, before she was slain by the Nameless King.
