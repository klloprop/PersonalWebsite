(Without Mik)

We start the session with a cold open from Aster that explores Carmelios inner turmoil and his own magic. After the party leaves the graveyard with the information about an unguarded entrance into the Citadel, Carmelio takes a detour. He ends up in a scrapyard, surrounded by waste, metal and the shells of automatons. There he finds the siren head automaton that has attacked Akira. There he learns how to utilize his primordial engine more and how to connect to other automaton, if only for a limited time. 

The session starts back at the safehouse, with the party preparing for the heist, illusion spells are being cast and preparation are made. 

Noon approaches Arneal to talk about his recent behavior. Arneal eventually apologizes to Noon.

Wes and Akira have a small heart to heart and say goodbye, as Wes is staying behind in the safehouse.

Reverie checks in on Carmelio and they share a small conversation about Carmelios state of mind. Afterwards Reverie uses the Sea of Knowledge to determine Carmelios original purpose. She learns that Carmelio was initially designed only with the primordial engine and was meant to be a shell for Aliana Starwielder. Reverie sees that the mages insert a small black shard of unknown material that apparently contains a part of Aliana Starwielder into Carmelios engine. A reaction occurs but the operation overall seems unsuccessful.

After Reverie returns, she informs Carmelio of what she has learned and Carmelio takes it as you would expect. 

Then the heist starts and the group makes their way to the Citadels back kitchen entrance. They enter the main chamber without many issues and see Mavari and Ramira briefly. Then the party is approached by a group of recruits they proceed to deceive about their presence here.
Make their way up to the dormitories, they come upon a group of paladins blocking the way. Reverie and Akira flirt with the paladins, allowing the group to slip past.
The following stairs lead them up to the Upper Ring. Once there, an Elven Firefly security alarm swarms them. On the spot Sasha creates a magical solution to mark their presence to these fey. 
Choosing to spend as little time on this layer as possible, they move further up the tower. There the group finds the door to the lower artifact chamber unlocked and the room beyond empty. Then they are attacked by a stone effigy of a slain hydra. Sasha finds and dissolves the petrified heart of the hydra, the very same artifact that animated that statue.
Dorchiot and Arkus manage to escape the cell, but they have triggered some silent alarm and now have to evade Hallorn the faceless. 
To escape his wrath they crawl into a small shaft that contains the alarm-bell rope. They climb down until the shaft ends, they step outside on the Upper Ring, as the alarm rings out throughout the citadel.
