(Without Kenny and Mik)

Our little group of misfits finds themselves with free time on their hands. Promptly Carmelio decides to interrogate the book, but upon opening finds that it has been torn to shreds from the inside, almost killing the entity trapped within. A short conversations answers few questions and raises many more, apparently something has been trapped inside of this book a very long time ago and is a sort of keeper of history, additionally the book insistently claims that Carmelio himself has torn the book apart. 

Following these events the group leaves Lucius and Shuni and splits up. Arkus, with Noon and Mask in tow, aims to build a new weapon, a grenade that utilizes the iron dust to destroy fey. Upon revealing this information to Noon and Mask a tense argument breaks out, resulting in Mask leaving and Arkus and Noon following their plans. 

The other part of the group head to the Vortex Spa and enjoy a warm soak, as Carmelio asks how one can apologize to someone that has you have wronged deeply. Nailo and Reverie may haps read into this situation.

As the time in the Spa draws to a close and Arkus has returned home to start tinkering away Mask waits in an alley next to the Spa. The blue dragonborn appears and a fight ensues, but it is quickly clear that Mask is heavily outmatched and is initially only saved by a yellow lightning bolt. The Dragonborn reveals that they are called the "Ivory Might". The fight continues as magical darkness spreads across the plaza and dust is being kicked up by a starting dust storm. 

As the party exits the Spa, they find Mask dead, lying in the streets, a tiefling girl desperately trying to keep his soul close by and Golden Knights of the Brightstrider Dominion securing the area. After initial discussions Carmelio approaches, recognizing the girl and another tiefling in the group of paladins. A tense moment later, a flashback to Carmelios birth and the dust clears, as a shockwave of Carmelios Magic surges through the girl and Mask. 

It takes a moment, but eventually Mask draws breath once more. 

How will our heroes cope with the many familiar faces in this complicated situation? How will they escape the attention of the dominions that now surely rests upon them and what will happen next?
