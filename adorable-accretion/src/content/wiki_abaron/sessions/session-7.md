(Without Mik, partially without Four and Hime)

The party decides to rest before risking an interaction with the automatons in the village. During the rest Mask and Sasha bond a little and fish for explosive crystals, just before Sasha uses his uncanny abilities to peek into Masks uncertain future. Carmelio and Arkus have a heartfelt conversation about the role of each other in their lives. Reverie and Nailo try to build trust, but are stopped by their own problems.

The next day they approach the tree and the automatons. After a tense start to their interaction, Carmelio is beckoned towards the tree. A voice erupts as the metal statue starts moving, asking Carmelio what he seeks. The party soon realizes that they are being tricked by fey magic and try to make a run for it. A giant moth emerges and cuts of their path, before transforming into a Archfey blocking their path and as she explains her hatred toward topside. Just before the session ended, another moth-fey landed beside the Archfey, pulling out a rapier.
