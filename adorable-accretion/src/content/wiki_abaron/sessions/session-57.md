**Sea of Knowledge:** This is the ebb and tide of time, that which washes away memory. Places that are about to be forgotten for good is where that sea rises through the ground and consumes. If a place is truly and totally forgotten, then it ceases to exist, it cannot be rediscovered or refound. It becomes part of the Sea of Knowledge. The Observer, Servas, is constantly working against this tide, keeping the universe whole against times corruption.
