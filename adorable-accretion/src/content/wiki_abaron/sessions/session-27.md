(Without Mik and Kenny)

A short but intense cold open has us following Ivory and how he stands in the background in every interaction, scared to do be a burden.

The group manages to avoid the Brightstriders who are now swarming Aneridge. The party decides to lay low for a while, before returning to the safehouse, however, once they do return to the safehouse Brighstriders are swarming the streets and buildings, raiding them one by one. Deeming this situation as dangerous the party decides to skip the safehouse and pursue their other plan directly. They successfully leave Anderidge and make their way down to the Crossroads. Even as the Heroes approach the final stop before the Caverns they somehow manage to evade the Brightstriders detection, however, not without drawing suspicion. 

The session ends with the party heading into the Caverns after many hours of travelling.
