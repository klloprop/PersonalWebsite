Aaron and Reverie are sharing some time together after their first kiss, the full events can be seen in the Roleplay thread called "Idiots in Love". Vhaerun and Nailo have a long conversation about their past, about Nailos fears and responsibilities and together. Eventually they form a plan to support the penumbra from the outside, in turn helping Nailo regain some confidence. They plan to scout out and disrupt the Brightstrider movements from the outside, while trying to establish a channel of communication with Menzo.

Arkus visits the local police station before leaving Raphia for a while. There he seeks out the holding cell and pays the bail for a tiefling called Ivvory. It turns out Ivvory and Arkus go way back and Ivvory has been locked away for some small made up reason. Now finally free again they catch up quickly and decide that Ivvory will join the rest of the group.

As the Session ends everyone arrives at the designated spot to leave Raphia for a long while.
