Our heroes arrived in Narthwith for various reasons, just as a speech by the whistleblower by the tabaxi Mask was starting. Learning disturbing secrets about the dominions, some of which may be very personal to our heroes. The speech was cut short by bursting wood and screams, automatons emerged and attempted to kill all present. 

As fate would have it our heroes and the tabaxi Mask escaped and found themselves in the forest not far from Narthwith. The automaton threat is not yet over, only evaded momentarily, however, our heroes will also need to find a way to deal with the fast approaching storm.
