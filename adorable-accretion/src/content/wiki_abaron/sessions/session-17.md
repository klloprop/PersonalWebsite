(Missing Mik and Ghost, additionally its the devilish Easter Weekend so everyone was kinda out of it)

As the session starts Carmelio hears a voice, something stirs within him, that entity seems to be waking up. A short and confusing conversation reveals that the entity speaks common, with a notable but unknown accent and is confused about the current situation, saying it does not recognize nor has it created Carmelio.

Noon receives her armor from the blacksmith.

While there are roughly 50 villagers of all age groups are present in the town hall Akira sees many more people here, not yet realizing she is seeing the spirits of the dead. 

A courier enters the town hall, completely drenched by the storm that is raging outside. Apparently the courier is hurt quite badly, but before he collapses he tells the assembly that the edge of the storm is not far and that they could reach it if they make a run for it. 

After some discussion and inspecting the strength of the building, the party decides to hunker down in the town hall and focus on keeping the building intact. Soon the undead stir outside as 5 powerful undead start attacking the town hall. It becomes clear that the undead can sense the living and start trying to break down the doors. As an offensive countermeasure the party moves to open the doors and fight them in the doorway. Nailo fires of powerful lightning bolts, as Akira and Noon step outside to keep the undead from moving into the building. Akira makes use of her new blade and shows off her amazing swordsmanship, as Noon utilizes a new magical ability, forming a dense blizzard around her. With two powerful strikes Noon destroys one of the undead, a cervitaur-zombie. In that fight however, both Akira and Noon get hurt very badly and Noon gets cursed by the undead cervitaur. 

Most of the undead are defeated using good party tactics and the doors are closed again, shielding everyone from the storms influence. Mask continues to coordinate the villagers and after 5 hours they finally outlast the storm, the town hall, mostly, still intact.
