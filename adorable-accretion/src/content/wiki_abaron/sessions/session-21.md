(Without Mik, Kenny and Ghost)

We start in the safehouse apartment with Xia, Nailos siblings, Astrea and Aeron, and the commander of the Shadowguard, Kyordal Caulssinn. In this safe space Reverie uses the Sea of Knowledge to determine why the Elder knight returned to Aneridge now. This vision reveals that the Elder knight is in contact with some mage from the Starwielder dominion, they discuss the 'Ivory threat'. Upon the return of the Elder Knight, the former Golden General, Guldor Keetruae, returns to the Golden Knights to oversee the training of their newest Recruits. Technomancers. 

After sharing this information with those present, Reverie tries to contact Aaron but her Earring breaks and starts oozing black oil-like liquid. Fearing for Aaron Reverie wants to use the Sea of Knowledge again immediately, to know what is happening, however, the rest of the party stop her. This chain of events causes Reverie to break down crying. Noon notices that a single tear running down Reveries cheek seems to resemble that black oil-like liquid that is still oozing out of her earring. In an attempt to dispel whatever this presence is, Noon uses her primordial Magic to summon Everfrost on Reverie, shielding her from all outside magical influence for a moment.

Whatever plagued the Eladrin vanished, but after a short while the exhaustion and cold got to Reverie. However shortly after Noon casts the Everfrost, Carmelio feels a sudden strong pain in his chest and the magic momentarily stops flowing through his metallic shell. 

Using the orb that Nailo has received from his mother, Xia, he contacts his parents and is initially greeted by a confused but quickly overjoyed Percy. After a few moments Mel and Mala join the conversation and express how thankful they are that Nailo is safe. The current situation in Penumbra is discussed and Mel assures Nailo that everything is under control and that their people are strong.

Nailo re-introduces Vhaerun to his parents and they show, a somewhat expected, reservation for the young drow. Surprisingly, Mel, thinking back to his complicated history with his husband Mala, shows support for Nailo, but still urges him on to tread carefully.  

Nailo brings up that he learned that one of his fathers visited a witch and made a deal. Percys, expression sours and he leaves Mel and Mala to explain the complicated story to their son. Mala reveals that the curse, placed upon their firstborn child, would have killed Nailo at birth. To give Nailo a chance at life Mala made a deal with the witch to shoulder halve of the curse himself, in return he gives up on the dream that people will accept him for what and/or for who he is. Nailo learns that the curse affects them in the same way. 

After this tense conversation resolves Mel leaves to tend to the war. Mala reveals that he does not share his husbands enthusiasm and tells Nailo of a secret plan to overwhelm the Brightstriders by luring devils from the hells to Penumbra. Nailo is very open to this idea. 

Seeing the state of the group Xia declares that everyone should rest and bigger endeavors could wait until the next morning. 

To use the rest of the afternoon the party splits up. Reverie rests in the safehouse, accompanied by Arkus, Ivory, Noon, Carmelio, Mask, Aeron and Caulssinn. Carmelio inspects the pain, looking for damage in his system, quickly revealing that his primordial engine has been completely encased in jagged everfrost. Noon gets to work to remove that anti-magical ice from her friend. 

Nailo, Vhaerun research the topology of Umbra and how it connects to the hells below, learning more about the twisting eldritch caverns that prevent the devils from wandering to the surface. The Caverns are almost impossible to navigate and seem to be the main domain of Nocros, the Primordial of the Deep.
In this research Vhaerun expresses concern for Nailos plan to lure devils to Penumbra, however, this moment of concern does not last long as the two share a kiss.
 
Akira makes her way into Anerdige looking for an art store. By chance she comes upon a smaller store, displaying many recent artworks. She asks the older halfling to draw her husband as she describes him from memory. As they sit in the backroom of the small shop Akira notices a drawing depicting her house and herself in front of it. She quickly learns that her husband, by some miracle, has come to the same artist to commission something to remember Akira by. Akira asks the halfling lady to finish the drawing of her husband from her own memory of him and she waits in the store for Wes to pick up the drawing. 

Many hours pass until the sun is falling below the horizon. Finally Wes, in an Ivory dominions uniform, accompanied by a large cloaked figure arrives at the store. Akira swoops up Wes, who is overjoyed but quickly expresses concern, begging Akira to leave. Instead the Cervitaur, swoops up Wes and starts running away with him. The cloaked figure is revealed to be an automaton, sounding some sort of alarm and following Akira, biting at the cervitaurs body. 

Patrolling Paladins intervene and allow for a heavily injured Akira to flee with Wes. They leave the inner ring of Aneridge, cross the Bridges and finally in the out ring stop. They share a kiss as Arneal and Pandora spot them and join the pair. 

We end the session as we see Faena, they hunt incarnate, sitting in nearby brush having followed the smell of blood and to her own surprise, having found who she was looking for.
