A buzzling town in the Red Forest. A enourmous oak marks the center of this town, its branches protecting the town from the dangers of the storms.  

Narthwith is halve a days travel away from Oakheart.

The iron oak, also called oakheart, towers at the center of this town. Historically it has been cared for by both mortal druids and fey but in recent time the fey have dissappeared and with the leyline shift the druids cant properly care for the oakheart. 

Due to the recent development the oakheart has started dying and petrifying, but its body still shields the town from the many magical storms that ravage the area.
