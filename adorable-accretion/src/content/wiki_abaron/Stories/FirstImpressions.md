**TW:** Bullying, Depression, Death, Broken arm, implied amputation, yuri (Faena x Merith)

The hulking figure of the nameless king is dressed in elegant black robes, towering over the assembly. His face is a swirling mass of clouds and storm with yellow lights occasionally visible from within. His voice thunders across the banquet, "Welcome one and all to this ball. In a moment the dance will start, but we will wait for the remaining guests to arrive, before the main event. Thank you." His voice barely interrupts the many conversations in the crowded room.
*No respect* Faena thinks to herself.
___ 
Faena finally escapes onto a balcony. She could only refuse so many invitations to dance before she had to give in. 
"The famed daughter of nature and hunt" she quietly mocks one of the suitors. 
She had to endure stumbling over her own feet for a few minutes, before she could finally make an escape. Her fur is a mess and her hands tug at the tight dress, trying to wrangle it back into position. 
Happy with her repaired appearance she finally takes a deep breath of fresh night air. 
*Events like this have a way of choking you*

She barely manages to take in the night before a group of fey join her on the balcony.
"Oh isn't that the famed daughter of Mother Nature and Father Hunt?"
"Yeah, shame she didn't inherit any of the good qualities from her parents."
Faena holds back a growl as she turns to face the trio.
"Tell me famed daughter, what ARE you trying to be? You could at least try to life up to one of your parents reputation." they mock.
Faenas voice isnt as strong as it should have been, "I am the hunt. If you have doubts i'll graciously give you a head start." It takes everything to try to remain calm. They want her angry, they want the drama.
"Did you see her dance? She can barely put a foot in front of the other, I actually favor your chances." they muse to each other.
"She doesnt tend to the dryads or leads the hunt, what WOULD her parents think? Its a shame that their daughter turned out like *this*"
"They could have at least taught her to dance."
Their laughter drowns out the music leaking onto the balcony, as tears threaten in the young feys eyes. 
"They- they tried-" Faenas voice shakes.

"If you are so confident, then what are you waiting for?" A new voice cuts through the laughter.
"What?" the trio turns to the new voice.
"Your chances are favored by your friends, come on, **run**." a blue dragonborn in a elegant and revealing dress steps forward from the balcony doors, "put some meaning behind those hollow words."
Uncertainty arises as the intruder steps between them, "Then you can experience first hand what she is and isn't capable off."
Silence falls over the balcony, as the trio exchanges nervous looks.
"As if we would engage in such primitive tests." They exchange some exasperated comments before they eventually leave. 
Merith closes the balcony doors behind them, finally allowing quiet to settle in this place.


"I don't need help" Faena starts, but her shaky voice betrays her faux confidence, "and certainly not from a mortal."
Meriths eyes search Faenas face before answering, "You don't need to be an asshole only because everyone else is an asshole" Merith leans against the railing next to her, her gaze focused on the distant landscape.
"Ah the superior act." Faena hisses.
"Come now I wanted to help you"
"And what do you want in return, mhh?" It always goes like this. Quid pro quo. Kindness is but an illusion, "I've never asked for your help."
Merith remains silent for a moment, before answering, "I did not do this to garner a favour, but if I could ask for one anyways... How about you bless me with a conversation." she smiles turning towards Faena, "you caught my eye more than once tonight, I want to see who is behind that pretty face."
"Tsk" Faena doesn't reward her with even a glance.
"What brings you to this ball then?" Still no answer, "I would think there are better places to brood." Merith teases.
"Im not brooding, im just-" she sighs, finally relaxing a bit, "I don't like this whole owing a favor thing, never turns out well. Everyone here is a snake."
"Vultures" Merith corrects, "Snakes don't follow bigger animals to harass them."
Faena finally turns to face the dragonborn, "You really thought about this huh?"
"Not much else to do at these events." Merith muses, "And besides no favor, no deal. "
"So im free to leave?" Faena raises an eyebrow
"Are you craving the dance floor that much?" 
"Urgh, 10 owlbears couldn't drag me back there" Faena answers, "I guess this is favourable after all. First real conversation of the night." she smirks at Merith.
"Dancing isn't as bad, once you know the basics. I could show you" 
"That's the culmination of your plan? All this to ask me to dance with you?" her gaze has turned playful, "but we are **not** going back inside."
"Mhh," Meriths hands traces along the railing, before making eye contact "I like the idea of having you all to myself."

___

Laughter and playful conversation echo plentiful into the night. 
"Come on, Im can't be that bad" Faena pouts, but a smile prevails.
"I've never seen someone actually have two left feet." Merith laughs, holding onto the railing for support.
"Hey, I only stepped on your feet like twice in the last attempt!" Faena steps closer, her hands finding Meriths. 
"You know your first dance now" Merith leans in closer, "I can teach you more, in due time."
"Hehe, I'm guessing you'd enjoy that"

Their moment is cut short by the sound of crashing glass and screams erupting from inside the hall.
"What is-?" Faena starts but is cut off by another scream.
"Maybe the main event started" Merith reasons, but her tone reveals doubts.

Faena is the first to move and open the balcony doors. The inside reveals utter carnage. Blood stains the walls, bodies litter the dance floor and various monstrous forms roam freely. 
Merith stifles a scream at the sight, her hand reaching for Faenas.
"Fey killing fey" Faena whispers, "We need to get out of here."

Suddenly, the ground shakes, the earth itself is parting. Someone is fighting for their life.
The building is pulled apart, the wood groaning before splintering and the bricks giving way to the night sky. The ground beneath the pair gives way, making them crash into the ground floor.

It takes them a moment to reorient, somehow they didn't get hurt, *yet*.
"What is happening?" Meriths whispers.
"No idea, but-" 
Another loud crack. Before Faena can finish speaking the rest of the ceiling gives way.
This time Merith is faster. She lunges forward just barely preventing Faena from being crushed. They tumble into the room as more debris fall, until the carnage eventually stops. The crumbling sound of bricks is replaed by Meriths screams. Her arm got caught under a large piece of rubble, crushing it. 
Faena shoots to her feet, fear rising inside of her, as suddenly a enormous figure looms over them.
The nameless king, a storm barely contained by jagged black armor, dragging a large blade, beholds them. The air is filled with crackling potential as the being reaches for Merith.
"NO" Faena yells as she jumps to interpose. 
The nameless king stops for a brief moment before reaching around Faena and lifting the large rubble off of Merith.

"You better hurry home" he thunders, "You weren't meant to be here, little hunt."
The king turns and starts walking down a hallway. 
As Faena turns her heart sinks. Meriths arm is in bad shape. 
"Do- Don't move." Faena instructs, but Merith is barely conscious. 
Carefully and slowly Faena lifts Merith, trying to support her arm.

___
Merith can remember that they were running, that Faena carried her, but not much else beyond that.

As the dragonborn awakes she is met by Faena. The, currently sleeping, fey kneels next to the bed, her head resting on the mattress.
Its morning now, sun is breaking through the window into the room. The space is small, a wooden interior with a random assortment of small knick knacks scattered througout. 
Merith already knows before she feels the ache. She reaches for her elbow only to find a stump.
*Classic*

Bringing the remaining appendage into view she starts studying it. It is bandaged nicely, somebody took great care with this. 
As Merith looks up again she is met with a very much awake Faena, tears streaming down her face. 
"I tried to safe your arm, and- and-" The tears are drowning her voice, "The witch wouldn't help, not without some bullshit deal and-"
"Faena" Merith tries to get through to the fey, her hand reaching for her face, "Its okay, I regret nothing"
"But, but" Faena stutters, before leaning into her hand, silence falls between them. 
"You didn't tell me you were a fey." Faena eventually says.
"Does it matter? You spend the evening with me regardless."
"I guess... OH im so sorry, let me get you water and-"
Merith interrupts her once more, grabbing Faenas wrist, "Wildcat,", their eyes meet, "stay".
Before Faena realises what is happening Merith has pulled her into the small bed with her, her remaining arm wrapped tightly around the hunts waist.
Faena slowly allows her body to uncurl and wrap around Merith, eventually getting comfortable.
Quiet purrs and chuckles are shared between them.

"Why wildcat?" Faena eventually whispers, but Merith has already drifted back to sleep.
A smile forms as Faena closes her eyes to join her in restful slumber.
