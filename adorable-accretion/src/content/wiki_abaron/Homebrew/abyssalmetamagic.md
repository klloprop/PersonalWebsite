Dark metamagic practiced only in the abyss... until now. Nailo, with the help of Nazira, has gained access to some abyssal metamagic options:

**Entangled (2 Sorcery Point):** All saving throws caused by this spell are made with disadvantage. No matter where the target of your spell is, you also get hit by the effect and have to make a respective saving throw. You make the saving throw without advantage or disadvantage.

**Borrow Mind (3 Sorcery Points):** You may ignore the concentration requirement for a spell. A creature within 60 feet that you can see concentrates on target spell instead. That creature makes all saves as it would usually. The creature may not choose to stop concentrating on your spell. You cannot target a creature that is already concentrating on a spell or effect. You may use another Metamagic option after this one.

**Perfect Entropy (Reaction, 1 Sorcery Point):**
When you would make a saving throw, you may choose another creature to make the save instead. Damage is applied normally.

**Void (2 Sorcery Points + halve current HP):** Feed your magic with your own vital force. Loose halve of your current HP (rounded down), this damage cannot be reduced in any way. Copy the next spell you cast twice, if it only targets 1 creature or object. If the next spell targets two or more creatures (AOE etc) copy that spell once. Choose new targets for the copies

**Embrace Chaos (X Sorcery points):** For each sorcery point spend increase the spell level by 1. After you cast the spell, roll a number of D6 equal to the sorcery points spend, take that much damage. This damage can trigger a concentration check.

**Consume (1 Sorcery Point):** Consume a living being to empower your next spell (free action), the living creature must be willing. Its soul will be consumed and the target cannot be revived in any way. Your next spell deals maximum damage on all dice and the amount of total damage is doubled.
The consumed creature must at least be CR 3.
