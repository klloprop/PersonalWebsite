This is your safe haven, your workshop and your home. 
### Bastion Level - **Level 1**

Your bastion level increases as you unlock upgrades. It represents the overall power and progress of your home base. You might unlock outposts later on that act as mini bastions.

# Bastion Facilities
## Shadowguard (Level 2)
Trained elite warriors of Menzo are available to you. If excess warriors are available, these can be send as a supporting element on expeditions (A expedition needs to be lead by a Hero).

Upgraded via Training ground.

## Graveyards (Level 1; MAX)
A monument to the war of light and shadow honors the dead, who are resting in their graves below a field of flowers.

Another lesser known graveyard, dedicated to the fallen brightstriders sits outside of Menzos walls.

This helps the people cope and come to terms with the horrors. Increases the happiness of the civilization slightly. 
## Vital Infrastructure (Level 1)
The bastions basic needs are met, chances of riots and revolutions are reduced. Increases population happiness.

Can heal any person within a 2 weeks of treatment.
Cannot cure curses.

Upgraded via extended infrastructure (Requires Menzo level 2).
## Hot Springs (Level 1)
Increases happiness and wealth.

Enables some fun downtime activities.
## Training grounds (Level 1)
Provides ability TRAIN CHARACTER

Enables construction of defensive and offensive weapons and automatons.

# Downtime Structure
Upgrades happen in the background as time passes in the world.

However the party might have downtimes in Penumbra in this case we follow this structure.

```Note: Heroes are all characters listed in the Bastion overview.``` 
1. Activate Bastion abilities/choose expeditions.
2. Choose upgrades for Menzo.
3. Choose which hero (if anyone) leads which upgrade.
	- Heroes can optionally lead upgrades, which consume the majority of the downtime for the upgrades duration. In return the Hero who led the upgrade may describe the result and possible get a small bonus upgrade flavored by their description.
4. Round of Actions: Every PC decides how to spend their downtime (travel, activities etc.)
5. Whenever a downtime goal or a upgrade goal is completed a short RP scene happens in which everyone (physically present) can join
6. Choose new upgrades
7. Choose new downtime activities/update downtime activities
8. Ask for a short RP scene
9. Jump to 5
