# Cleric: Cosmic Domain

Clerics of the Cosmic Domain (sometimes called Clerics of Spacetime) are a new addition to the varied pantheon of clerics that came about with the discovery of Arcana thousands of years ago. With that came understanding of the flow of time and the matter of cosmic space. Clerics of this domain study the rules of the cosmos, which by extension are the rules of the gods, as they created the cosmos we live in. This order attracts those drawn to knowledge and wonder — they may be the most travelled of all clerics, looking to document some of the world's most weird and wonderful godly and arcane artefacts. The magic they have found with their deities is new, and some consider it dangerous.

Clerics of the Cosmic Domain can be found side by side with wizards and sorcerers, understanding the ways of the world and the magic that permeates through it. As a result, they are sometimes met with suspicion or animosity by clerics of more ancient traditions. Where they are welcome, Cosmic Clerics are often known as great educators or sometimes even detectives, as they are most often driven to find facts that are not evident to the layman.

## Cosmic Influence

*1st-level Cosmic Domain feature*

You understand the nature of space — not just that people occupy it, but how they physically and spiritually interact with it and one another. As an action, you choose a number of willing creatures within 30 feet of you (this can include yourself) equal to your proficiency bonus. You create a magical bond among them for 10 minutes or until you use this feature again. While any bonded creature is within 30 feet of another, the creature can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw it makes. Each creature can add the d4 no more than once per turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Implement of the Cosmos

*1st-level Cosmic Domain feature*

You gain proficiency in the Insight, Performance, or Persuasion skill (your choice).

---

## Channel Divinity: Flow of Time

*2nd-level Cosmic Domain feature*

You can use your Channel Divinity to slow time for an enemy for a brief moment. As an action, you can move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks, and when you move within 5 feet of any other creature during this action, you can restore a number of hit points to that creature equal to 2d6 + your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1 hit point). A creature can receive this healing only once whenever you take this action.

---

## Channel Divinity: Fractal Bond

*6th-level Cosmic Domain feature*

Your understanding of the folding of space in the cosmos helps protect your party just by them being in your presence. When a creature affected by your Cosmic Influence feature is about to take damage, a second bonded creature within 30 feet of the first can use its reaction to teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the first creature. The second creature then takes all the damage instead.

---

## Potent Spellcasting

*8th-level Cosmic Domain feature*

You add your Wisdom modifier to the damage you deal with any cleric cantrip.

> **Optional Feature — Blessed Strikes** *(replaces Potent Spellcasting)*
> You are blessed with the might of the whole cosmos, channeled through your god. When a creature takes damage from one of your cantrips or weapon attacks, you can also deal 1d8 radiant damage to that creature. Once you deal this damage, you can't use this feature again until the start of your next turn.

---

## Expansive Influence

*17th-level Cosmic Domain feature*

The benefits of your Cosmic Influence and Fractal Bond features now work when the creatures are within 60 feet of each other. Moreover, when a creature uses Fractal Bond to take someone else's damage, the creature has resistance to that damage.
