## Academies
There are various academies within these lands, most of them are associated with one of the great Dominions that rule these lands. 

Little is known about the inner workings of the Academies, you only know that they are secluded and their doors only open to students, teachers and invited guests.

One of the few times the academies pupils travel the lands is in their yearly search for "gifted", who are then brought to the academies for further evaluation and education.
